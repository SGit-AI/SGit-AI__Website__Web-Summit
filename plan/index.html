The plan

Everything worked backwards from the summit, 9 to 12 November 2026 in Lisbon. From the team brief, part two and the plan of 26 October. Goals in the plan's order: usage first, the sale after. This is a plan: nothing on it has happened unless its status says so.

The design track, worked backwards

T-shirts, hoodies, cards for everyone, flyers, a banner and stickers: each has to be designed, approved, ordered, printed and delivered before 9 November, so each gets the date its design must be approved and the date it must be ordered, from the printers' published lead times. Every printed item carries a code that leads to a page. The designer decides; the designer agent produces.

Being worked out.

From a voice memo to a follow-up, within the hour

  1. Something is recorded: a voice memo after a conversation, a photo of a business card, a note to an agent, a code scanned at our stand.
  2. The owner's agent picks it up at its next wake, within the hour.
  3. The record is updated: the company and the person in the owner's personal CRM (private), the conversation in its timeline, the next step; the team view on this site shows only the owner, the status and the next step.
  4. An asset is made: a page for that company, with what was discussed and what we can do for them.
  5. A follow-up is drafted in the person's own email, waiting for them.
  6. The person sends it. No email goes out on its own.

What we ask each person for their card

  • What do you want to get out of Web Summit, in one line?
  • Which companies, sectors or people do you want to own on the map? Which do you already know?
  • Which conversations will you lead, and what are you not doing?
  • Where are you before the summit (Algarve, UK, Lisbon), for meetups and deliveries? (kept private)
  • Which agent do you work with today, and which email account should your email agent run in?
  • How do you like to receive a brief: a page, a voice note, a call, a message? How much context, and how fast do you usually reply?
  • What may your card on this site say, and may it carry your name? (nothing is published without your yes)

To kickstart, in the first meetings

  • Agree owners for the first 10 to 20 companies on the company map, starting with priority 1.
  • Fill each person's role card from the questions above, and say yes or no to being named.
  • Set the design budget, name who talks to the designer, and agree the items, quantities and delivery addresses (privately); the order-by dates on this page are the deadline.
  • Pick the first three assets to make (a page each for a company we want to meet), and who sends the link.
  • Agree the daily rhythm in Lisbon: the debrief time, and that every memo or card goes to the owner's agent within the hour.
  • Agree what we sell and show: the five-minute path to value for each product, as the plan asks.
