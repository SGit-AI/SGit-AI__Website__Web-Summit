Already too late on standard service

  • Vistaprint UK 'Express' (4 business days): last possible order is TOMORROW Mon 2 Nov 20:00 for Fri 6; after that only Rush (2 days) or Next Day remain.
  • Hoodies via Vistaprint (PT and UK): not express-eligible, so ordering Mon 2 arrives ~Tue 10 Nov at best. Pre-travel hoodies need a local express printer.
  • Roll-up banner via Vistaprint (PT and UK): not in either fast-delivery list -> standard only -> too late.
  • Embroidery of any garment this week: digitising + production makes it unrealistic; use print (DTG/DTF/vinyl).

Codes on print

  • Url rule: Encode the shortest stable HTTPS URL we control: https://web-summit.sgit.ai/team/<id>/ (about 35-40 characters -> a small QR, version 3, 29x29 modules, at error correction M). Do not use a third-party shortener or a 'dynamic QR' service that can expire or be re-pointed; if we want analytics, add a short query such as ?src=card.
  • Minimum size: Business card: at least 20 x 20 mm of code (22-25 mm preferred) plus the quiet zone. A6/A5 flyer: 25-30 mm. Sticker: 20 mm minimum. Roll-up: width >= scanning distance / 10 (about 15 cm for 1.5 m).
  • Quiet zone: At least 4 modules of empty space on all sides (on a 22 mm, 29-module code that is ~3 mm); never let it touch the trim or a background pattern.
  • Contrast: Dark modules on a light, plain background; black (100% K) on white is safest; at least ~4:1 contrast; never inverted (light on dark), never on a gradient or photo. Brand colour is fine only if it is dark.
  • Error correction: Level M by default; use Q/H only if a logo is placed in the centre (and then make the code larger).
  • Format: Generate as vector (SVG/PDF/EPS), place at 100%, do not rasterise or resample; keep it out of the 3 mm trim/safe zone; avoid gloss/foil/spot UV over it.
  • Human fallback: Print the short URL as text under the code (e.g. web-summit.sgit.ai/team/dinis).
  • Test before ordering: ['Scan the final PDF on screen with at least one iPhone and one Android camera app.', 'Print the card at 100% on an office printer, cut to size, scan from 15-30 cm in normal indoor light and in dim light.', "Confirm each code opens the right person's page over mobile data (not office wifi) and the page loads fast.", 'Check every one of the 7 cards: a swapped QR is the most likely error.', "Check the supplier's on-screen proof again after upload (some tools resample images)."]

From a voice memo to a follow-up, within the hour

  1. Something is recorded: a voice memo after a conversation, a photo of a business card, a note to an agent, a code scanned at our stand.
  2. The owner's agent picks it up at its next wake, within the hour.
  3. The record is updated: the company and the person in the owner's personal CRM (private), the conversation in its timeline, the next step; the team view on this site shows only the owner, the status and the next step.
  4. An asset is made: a page for that company, with what was discussed and what we can do for them.
  5. A follow-up is drafted in the person's own email, waiting for them.
  6. The person sends it. No email goes out on its own.

What we ask each person for their card

  • What do you want to get out of Web Summit, in one line?
  • Which companies, sectors or people do you want to own on the map? Which do you already know?
  • Which conversations will you lead, and what are you not doing?
  • Where are you before the summit (Algarve, UK, Lisbon), for meetups and deliveries? (kept private)
  • Which agent do you work with today, and which email account should your email agent run in?
  • How do you like to receive a brief: a page, a voice note, a call, a message? How much context, and how fast do you usually reply?
  • What may your card on this site say, and may it carry your name? (nothing is published without your yes)

To kickstart, in the first meetings

  • Agree owners for the first 10 to 20 companies on the company map, starting with priority 1.
  • Fill each person's role card from the questions above, and say yes or no to being named.
  • Set the design budget, name who talks to the designer, and agree the items, quantities and delivery addresses (privately); the order-by dates on this page are the deadline.
  • Pick the first three assets to make (a page each for a company we want to meet), and who sends the link.
  • Agree the daily rhythm in Lisbon: the debrief time, and that every memo or card goes to the owner's agent within the hour.
  • Agree what we sell and show: the five-minute path to value for each product, as the plan asks.
