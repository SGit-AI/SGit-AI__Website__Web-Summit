# The videos

The team's RiskMandate videos (Silvio's visual explanation and the first RiskMandate Story), the storyboard for the 2-minute silent loop at our stand, the pipeline from idea to published video, and how the team and anyone else gives feedback.

Site version: v0.1.31

RiskMandate is technical, so a 20-second promo is not enough: we explain it with stories and visual explanations, with and without sound. Made by Silvio Switha's video pipeline; published here first, then on our video channels.

```cards
Reach, Mandate, Gap | reach-mandate-gap/index.html | 53 s. The visual explanation: what an agent can reach, what it was authorised to do, and the gap.
RiskMandate Stories: fifty new agents | fifty-new-agents/index.html | 2 min 44 s. Monday, fifty new agents, and the questions before you switch them on.
Storyboard: the 2-minute silent loop | storyboard-loop/index.html | For the screen at our stand: stop, explain, start a conversation.
```

## The pipeline {#pipeline}

| Step | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Idea | A question a customer asks, written as an issue on [the board](../briefs/index.html#issues) | anyone |
| 2. Storyboard | Scenes, on-screen text, the job of each scene, as a page like [the loop's](storyboard-loop/index.html) | Silvio, Indi (the message), Nimay (positioning) |
| 3. Make | Silvio's video pipeline and agents | Silvio, video@web-summit.silvio |
| 4. Review | The team's votes and comments (below), then a fix pass | everyone |
| 5. Publish | A page and an article here (what it shows, the key concepts), then YouTube, Instagram and LinkedIn | Silvio, Angelo or Jose (hosting) |
| 6. Learn | Views, comments and conversations it started, back into the next storyboard | Vicky, the journalist agent |
