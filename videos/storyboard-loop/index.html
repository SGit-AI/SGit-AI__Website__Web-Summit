Storyboard: the 2-minute silent loop for the stand

From Silvio's proposal of 2 November 2026, with the operator's notes. Three jobs: stop people walking past, explain the problem and RiskMandate's answer, and start a conversation. Rules: no sound; every scene carries its own caption and the RiskMandate name, so someone who arrives mid-loop still understands it; large text, readable from three metres; no scene longer than 20 seconds; the last frame is the invitation, then it loops.

#TimePicture (from)On-screen textJob
10:00-0:08The agent network lighting up (Reach, Mandate, Gap)Your company just switched on 50 AI agents.stop
20:08-0:22Monday: the team and the robot walk in (Stories)Before you switch them on: what can they reach? What did you authorise?stop
30:22-0:40The blue dome of Reach around the gold ring of MandateReach: what an agent can actually touch. Mandate: what you authorised. The gap is your risk.explain
40:40-0:55The team at the table: "Who's we?" (Stories)Building is not authorising. Someone must be accountable.explain
50:55-1:10The ABP card: Reach, Mandate, Gap, Barriers (Stories)An Agent Behaviour Policy: a factual record your teams can check.explain
61:10-1:25The new connector: same agent, different Reach (Stories)Then its Reach changed. The old decision doesn't cover it.explain
71:25-1:40One agent, then 10, 100, 1,000+, one record each (Reach, Mandate, Gap)Different teams. One checkable record.explain
81:40-1:52The robot looks at the cameraAcross 50 agents: what exposure are you taking on?convert
91:52-2:00RiskMandate logo, QR to /meet/Know their reach. Define their mandate. Control the gap. Talk to us here, or scan to book a demo.convert

A bar on every scene

A thin bar along the bottom, always on screen: RiskMandate · Agent Behaviour Policies for AI agents · scan to book a demo with the QR to /meet/. Whoever looks at any moment knows what this is and what to do.
