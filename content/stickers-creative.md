RiskMandate Web Summit Edition ... 2026, kind of like road trip kind of thing ... a set of stickers that people might want to take and put on their stuff ... and even the team might want to put on their laptops."* Written by conductor@web-summit.dinis; the four current stickers stay as the "corporate" set ([/stickers/](../index.html)).

## The idea {#idea}

**RiskMandate, Web Summit Edition: the road trip.** A set that reads like souvenirs from a trip to Lisbon in November 2026, every one carrying one RiskMandate idea (reach, mandate, gap, permission) as a joke a visitor gets in two seconds. Numbered as a collectible series, "1 of 12", so people come back to the stand for the rest.

## The rules that keep it ours {#rules}

- **Words, exactly as written below**; short (an image model misspells long text; for anything over six words, generate the art without text and set the words in the vector file afterwards).
- **The RiskMandate seal is never generated by the model.** Leave a clean round space where it goes; the designer places the supplied file (riskmandate.ai/brand) afterwards.
- **Colours lead with the brand:** green #1A7F5A, ink #161615, warm white #F7F6F2, caution gold #B45309; Lisbon's own colours (azulejo blue, tram yellow, terracotta) as accents.
- **No other brand:** no Web Summit logo, no third-party marks, no real people. "Web Summit Edition" in words only.
- **Positive and clever, never scary.** The joke is on agents with too much reach, not on anyone's company.
- **Printable as die-cut vinyl:** a flat design with a white outline border about 3 mm wide all round (the cut follows it), a plain transparent or white background, no tiny detail under 1 mm, generated at 2048 x 2048 px or larger.

## The prompts, ready to paste {#prompts}

Paste the shared start first, then one sticker's line. Ask for four variations; keep the best; ask for the same again "with the words larger and simpler" if any letter is wrong.

**Shared start:** *"Design a die-cut vinyl laptop sticker, flat vector illustration style, bold clean shapes, thick white outline border about 3 mm wide all round the shape, on a plain transparent background, centred, no drop shadow, high contrast, readable from one metre. Palette: deep green #1A7F5A, near-black #161615, warm white #F7F6F2, caution gold #B45309, with Lisbon accents (azulejo blue #1D4ED8, tram yellow #F2C230). No logos, no brand marks, no real people. Text must be spelled exactly as given, in a bold condensed sans-serif."*

1. *"A retro 1970s travel patch, round. Inside: the 25 de Abril suspension bridge over a river at sunset, a small friendly green robot driving a vintage convertible, holding a folded map labelled MANDATE. Around the rim: RISKMANDATE · WEB SUMMIT EDITION · LISBON 2026. Leave a small plain circle at the top for a logo."*
2. *"A vintage Lisbon tram ticket, tram yellow, a small tram 28 drawing at the left, perforated edge. Big text: AGENT PASS. Small text: VALID WITHIN MANDATE ONLY."*
3. *"A single Portuguese azulejo tile, blue and white, ornate border. In the centre, a horizontal bar: the left two-thirds solid green labelled AUTHORISED, the right third striped gold and black labelled THE GAP. Below: CONTROL THE GAP."*

6. *"A triangular warning road sign, red border, white inside, a simple black pictogram of a small robot walking confidently towards a cliff edge. Below the sign on a plate: AGENT CROSSING. Smaller: KNOW THEIR REACH."*

## From image to printer {#print}

1. Pick the winners (vote on the team's site like the cards).
2. The designer agent traces or cleans each chosen image, places the supplied RiskMandate seal where it was left, sets any long text in vector, and draws the cut line (CutContour, its own layer, 3 mm outside the art), as for [the four current stickers](../index.html).
3. Export press-ready PDFs (CMYK, 300 dpi at size, 2 mm bleed) and send them with the order ([the print order for ProntaPrint](../../print-order/index.html)).
