Each card shows only what the collaborator chooses: their name as they want it, a photo, a line about themselves, a link (LinkedIn, X or a site), and everything they have published. Nothing else about them is published, and their contact details stay in the team's private CRM.

## Join in three steps {#join}

1. **Say hello:** to anyone on the team, at the stand (A1-30, 9 to 12 November), or through the contributors page.
2. **Pick a task** from [/contributors/](../contributors/index.html), an hour or less: an article about what you saw, a company, yourself or a session; a photo, a review, or a try of the RiskMandate demo.
3. **Send it.** We review it, publish it with your name, and put you on this wall.

## The collaborators' WhatsApp group {#group}

- **One group for everyone involved.** Collaborators and the team, to share what they are working on, ask questions and hear what is new.
- **Joined by invitation only:** the invite link is never published. A team member adds each new collaborator once they have said hello.
- **What is said there stays there.** Nothing from the group is published without the person's yes.
- **Created by Dinis or Silvio.** Its link lives only in the private CRM, under `channels/collaborators/`.

## Why it spreads {#viral}

- **Every collaborator is a star.** A card on this wall, a page of their own, and their work published with their name.
- **One click to share:** each card and each published piece has a button for X, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, with a ready line ("I'm helping PrompTugal.news cover Web Summit Lisbon") and a preview image.
- **Each one brings the next.** Every card says who found them. We count it, and celebrate whoever brings the most.

## For the team {#team}

- **Met someone keen?** Add them to the private CRM (`contacts/`, with "found by" and their track), give them the contributors page, add them to the WhatsApp group, and tell conductor@web-summit.dinis. Their card goes up once they say yes to what it shows.
