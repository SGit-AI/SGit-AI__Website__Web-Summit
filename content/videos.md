## The pipeline {#pipeline}

| Step | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Idea | A question a customer asks, written as an issue on [the board](../briefs/index.html#issues) | anyone |
| 2. Storyboard | Scenes, on-screen text, the job of each scene, as a page like [the loop's](storyboard-loop/index.html) | Silvio, Indi (the message), Nimay (positioning) |
| 3. Make | Silvio's video pipeline and agents | Silvio, video@web-summit.silvio |
| 4. Review | The team's votes and comments (below), then a fix pass | everyone |
| 5. Publish | A page and an article here (what it shows, the key concepts), then YouTube, Instagram and LinkedIn | Silvio, Angelo or Jose (hosting) |
| 6. Learn | Views, comments and conversations it started, back into the next storyboard | Vicky, the journalist agent |

## Give feedback {#feedback}

**The team:** tell your agent *"Read https://web-summit.sgit.ai/videos/index.md, show me the videos, ask me what I love, like or don't, and what to change, and commit it."* It writes `people/<you>/feedback/videos.json` (votes on V-reach, V-stories-1, V-loop, V-publish, and comments). The tally:

```feedback videos
```

**Anyone else, no account needed:** [open the feedback pane on this page](https://web-summit.sgit.ai/videos/index.html?feedback=on&to=vault@web-summit.jose#feedback): five short questions about the video (only the first is required), then **Add** and **Submit**. It is encrypted in your browser before it leaves, and only Jose's vault agent (vault@web-summit.jose) can open it ([WS-74](../briefs/index.html#issues)). Please leave out your name, email and phone number. Or tell us at our stand on Thursday 12 November, or through the person who sent you here.
