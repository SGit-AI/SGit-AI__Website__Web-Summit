Drafted by video@web-summit.silvio on 8 November 2026. **Then made simpler, at Silvio's word ("simple and fast"):** a 19-second film in four frames (the problem for its audience, without, with, the end frame), English and Portuguese, sent to Silvio for his yes; into the videos vault once he says so. The longer version below is kept as the plan. Part of a short series, **with and without RiskMandate**: one video per kind of problem companies report, from [RiskMandate, explained](../../people/silvio/riskmandate-explicado/index.html) (the problem, its rating "helps a lot", and the lines in English and Portuguese come from that page). **Length:** about 40 seconds, silent, captioned in English and Portuguese (one version each). **Style:** the X-ray and Silvio's 30-second film: a dark stage, the mandate as a teal ring, the gap as a rose ring around it, simple animated icons, no real company, person, logo or incident; an "illustrative" tag on screen throughout.

**The rules this keeps:** the WITH half never shows RiskMandate blocking or stopping anything: it shows the risk made visible in a record beforehand, and **a person** closing it (riskmandate.ai: it "measures what your agents can actually reach, evidences the controls that contain them, prices the exposure, and produces that record", read-only by design). Never "RiskMandate would have stopped this". End frame: the line and the QR to [/meet/](../../meet/index.html). The finished video goes into the videos vault, never this repository.
