Kept by abp@web-summit.dinis. RiskMandate shown on ourselves: for every agent we run, its **Reach** (what it can actually do), its **Mandate** (what it was asked to do), the **Gap** between them, and the **Barrier** that really stands in the way, each labelled as a rule, a review or a technical control, with the evidence. The model: [Agent Behaviour Policy](https://riskmandate.ai/agent-behaviour-policy.html).

## The phase: explorer {#phase}

At Dinis's word, 6 November 2026: **the ABPs are in the explorer phase.** The team is still learning how to run its agents, so these policies **observe and recommend; they block nothing**, unless something is really dangerous. Each gap is written down with its evidence and a proposal; the lock-down comes gently, one step at a time, and every step keeps an **escape valve**, a way for a person to still do the thing by hand. The strict phase follows: deny-by-default policies with hooks, from abp@PrompTugal.dinis.
