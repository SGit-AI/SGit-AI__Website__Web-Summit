Drafted by video@web-summit.silvio on 5 November 2026 (WS-75), from [the list of next videos](../index.html#next), in the shape of [the loop's storyboard](../storyboard-loop/index.html). Every line uses words already on this site; a new claim about RiskMandate goes to Dinis first. **For:** buyers of agents and their security and risk teams. **Where:** LinkedIn and YouTube, and the stand's laptop. **Length:** about 60 seconds, captioned, the robot *Agent* from [fifty new agents](../fifty-new-agents/index.html).

| # | Time | Picture | On-screen text | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00-0:06 | A buyer at a desk; the vendor's agent arrives in a box, the robot inside | **You're buying an AI agent.** | stop |
| 2 | 0:06-0:14 | The box opens; cables run from the robot to email, files, payments, customer data | **Who's on the other side of it?** | stop |
| 3 | 0:14-0:24 | The blue Reach dome grows around the robot | **Reach:** what it can actually access or affect, once it's connected to your systems. | explain |
| 4 | 0:24-0:34 | A small gold Mandate ring inside the dome | **Mandate:** what your business authorises it to do. **The gap** is your risk. | explain |
| 5 | 0:34-0:44 | The buyer slides a card across to the vendor: Reach, Mandate, Gap, Barriers | **Ask for the record:** an Agent Behaviour Policy your teams can check. | explain |
| 6 | 0:44-0:52 | The card gains a signature line: authority, owner, conditions, review | **Who is authorising what?** A Licence to Operate says who, until when, and when it's reviewed. | explain |
| 7 | 0:52-1:00 | The robot looks at the camera; logo, QR | **Know their reach. Define their mandate. Control the gap.** | convert |

**What it must not say:** that RiskMandate checks or blocks the vendor's agent. It does not decide risk, give permission or block actions ([fifty new agents](../fifty-new-agents/index.html#concepts)): a record is not a control.

**Open:** who is the buyer (a person or a team)? Is the vendor in the picture, or only its agent?
