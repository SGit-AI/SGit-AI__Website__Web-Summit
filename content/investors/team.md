## The agents {#agents}

Each person runs their own agents, and the team runs shared ones (the conductor, a journalist, a pulse, security, design, development, behaviour policies, a project manager, mentors, the newsroom). The full list, with their schedules and prompts, is on [the agents page](../../agents/index.html); everything counted is on [everything on the team](../../team/everything/index.html). This page is kept by one of them, investors@web-summit.dinis.

## How the team works {#how}

Two decision meetings a day, commits as the record, a board of issues anyone can pick up, and a dashboard per person ([start](../../start/index.html#how)). The people bring their own clients and reasons to be there; the team helps each of them get what they came for ([the brief](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/BRIEF.md)).

**Not yet decided, and not ours to say:** who holds what in the company, and who joins it formally. That is Dinis's to decide with each person ([the investment](../../investors/investment/index.html)).
