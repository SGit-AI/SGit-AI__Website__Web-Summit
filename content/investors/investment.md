**No numbers here, on purpose.** Dinis, 6 November 2026: *"we're not going to make any projections based on revenue because we don't know."* A valuation, a round size and terms are for a conversation with each investor, not for a public page.

## What it is for {#for}

Dinis: *"the first 6 months to a year is about discovering the market, selling services, scaling up this agentic team, maybe selling customized versions of the agentic team for individuals, for teams."* So an investment pays for:

1. **Time for the team that is in place**, so the people can work on this rather than around it. Dinis has written that startups need time more than money ([Fund it with time](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/fund-it-with-time.html), 19 October 2026).
2. **Discovering the market by selling**: services, customised agentic teams, packages ([how we make money](../../investors/business-model/index.html)), with the asks counted and reported.
3. **Scaling the agentic team**: more agents, more people running them, and the tools that turn one-off work into packages.
4. **Making the customer's version real**: the first customised teams for individuals and teams, made repeatable.

It is not for a large fixed cost base: the costs are minimal by design, which is why Dinis expects the company to be profitable very fast (his expectation, not a projection).

## Rounds {#rounds}

Dinis spoke of **multiple rounds**, each as traction shows, rather than one large raise up front. His stance, written before this week: "The worst possible time to raise money is before you are profitable" ([The question is whether they miss it](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-question-is-whether-they-miss-it.html), 21 September 2026). The conversation he expects with an investor is, in his words, *"how much of [the company] do you want to have in stock."*

He has also written about other shapes than issuing new shares, such as an investor buying a founder's existing shares once the company is profitable ([Sell, don't issue](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/sell-dont-issue.html), 16 October 2026). Which shape fits which round is for him to decide with each investor.

## What an investor gets besides a share {#more}

- **Everything in the open**: the code, the decisions, the releases, the pitch's own history. Due diligence starts with reading.
- **Updates as we get traction**: these pages, the decks and their PDFs will be updated as things happen, and each change is a public commit.
- **The agentic team at work for them**, too: an investor or introducer gets a page of their own, made for them ([an example](../../investors/for-algarve-evolution/index.html)).

## Before anyone invests {#before}

Three things to settle, said plainly ([the company](../../investors/company/index.html#open)): the company's status at the official register; who owns and manages it; and which company sells what, between My Feeds and The Cyber Boardroom Limited. They are Dinis's to settle and these pages will say when they are.
