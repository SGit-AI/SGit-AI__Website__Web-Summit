## In one line {#line}

**We make and run agentic teams: people working with AI agents that have keys, budgets and written mandates, and we show the work in public before we sell it.**

## For whom {#who}

| Who | What they get | Where it is shown today |
|---|---|---|
| **An expert whose value does not scale** (a teacher, a consultant, a practitioner) | an agentic team that runs their website, their workflows and the work before and after each session with their clients, under rules they set | the first friendly, Daniela ([her brief](../../brief-daniela/index.html)) |
| **A team** (a startup, a practice, an event) | the same, for a team: a site, a board, a conductor, a briefing for each person, decisions on the record | this site, built for the Web Summit team ([start](../../start/index.html)) |
| **An organisation or an ecosystem** (an association, a region, a delegation) | a sourced picture of their world: pages, a map, a programme, made in days and corrected by them | [Algarve Evolution on PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/portugal/algarve-evolution/) |
| **A company that runs AI agents** | the controls to give each agent a mandate and see the gap: RiskMandate | [riskmandate.ai](https://riskmandate.ai/) and [our own policies](../../abp/index.html) |

## What makes it different {#different}

- **It is shown, not explained.** The buyer sees their own world, made by us, before any conversation about money. The team's strategy, in Dinis's words to the conductor: RiskMandate "has to be shown, not explained" ([the brief, part 3](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/BRIEF.md)).
- **Agents with mandates.** Every agent has its own key, a published behaviour policy and a person it answers to. The more the agents are constrained, the more useful they are and the more comfortable the person is (same source).
- **In the open.** The code is Apache-2.0, the text CC BY 4.0, the history public. A buyer can check everything before paying.
- **Fast.** This site and PrompTugal.news went from a brief to daily releases within a week; the release history is [public](../../admin/versions.html).

## What we are not {#not}

- **Not a model company.** The reasoning engine is Claude; we build the team, the rules and the work around it.
- **Not a platform you must move into.** No lock-in: the work lives in open formats, in the customer's own repositories and encrypted vaults. Dinis on sgit: "as soon as you move into rent extraction, you lose sight of it" ([sgit.ai/investors](https://sgit.ai/investors/index.md)).
- **Not a seller of coverage.** The newsroom's rule, kept by its customers' agent: a buyer can buy a service made for them, never what the publication says ([the monetisation map](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website/blob/dev/board/team/customers/monetisation-map.md)).
- **Not a promise of numbers.** We do not forecast revenue ([the investment](../../investors/investment/index.html)).
