## Why a small company in the Algarve {#why}

Low costs, a region that wants technology companies beyond tourism ([Algarve Evolution on PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/portugal/algarve-evolution/)), a part of the team there, and a European base for a team that sells its work anywhere.
