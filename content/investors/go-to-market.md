## The method {#method}

Dinis, in the plan for the summit: "create an asset, put it on a website, send a link" ([Web Summit 2026, the plan](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/web-summit-2026-the-plan.html), 26 October 2026). And from the strategy conversation of 1 November: the unlock is a real example in the other person's world, "here is what you do, here is what RiskMandate does for you" ([the brief](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/BRIEF.md)).

1. **Make something about them.** A sourced page, a map of their ecosystem, an analysis of their business through RiskMandate. Public facts only.
2. **Send a link.** A person sends it, from their own address.
3. **Listen.** The best sign is a request for a change. On 6 November Algarve Evolution did exactly that ([the story](../../investors/story/index.html#promptugal)).
4. **Ask for their wish list.** A prompt for their own AI interviews them and writes the list of what they would want built (PrompTugal.news's [wish-list prompt](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website/blob/dev/board/team/customers/wish-list/prompt-algarve-evolution.md)). It also shows them the agentic team at work.
5. **Sell what they asked for**, with a fixed scope; turn it into a package on the seventh ask ([how we make money](../../investors/business-model/index.html#seven)).

## Commercialisation {#commercial}

- **Fixed scope, fixed price, a change policy**, so a buyer never meets a surprise.
- **Payment links for menu items, invoices for named jobs**; a person sends both ([the monetisation map](https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website/blob/dev/board/team/customers/monetisation-map.md)).
- **Usage first, then the sale** ([the plan](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/web-summit-2026-the-plan.html)); and Dinis's test, "price it, then give it away": if people will not take it free, the price is not the problem ([Price it, then give it away](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/price-it-then-give-it-away.html), 2 October 2026).
- **Not too soon.** "If we try to sell them RiskMandate too soon, they're gonna go no" (the stand-up of 5 November, as the map quotes it): we start with what they asked for.

## What we will measure {#measure}

Not revenue forecasts. What we will report, as it happens: who asked for a change, who asked for more, the asks by kind (the rule of seven's ledger), what was sold, and what it taught us.
