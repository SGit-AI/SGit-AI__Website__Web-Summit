## Your goals, as you have stated them {#goals}

From [PrompTugal.news's page on Algarve Evolution](https://promptugal.news/portugal/algarve-evolution/), which you have read, and its sources:

- **Diversify the Algarve's economy beyond tourism**, and help established industries become more competitive.
- **Three pillars**: education, startups and SMEs, and the innovation ecosystem ([algarveevolution.pt](https://www.algarveevolution.pt/)).
- **Goals for 2032** for the companies supported, the startups created and the people reached (as reported by [Portugal Startup News](https://portugalstartupnews.com/2026/11/01/algarve-evolution-aims-to-support-500-companies-and-help-create-150-startups-by-2032-in-push-beyond-tourism/), 1 November 2026).
- **For your members**: "opportunities for collaboration, mentorship, and funding" ([algarveevolution.pt](https://www.algarveevolution.pt/)), through programmes such as Founder2Founder, the Algarve Digital Innovators network, the incubator and INOVA ALGARVE 3.0's AI consultancy for tourism SMEs.
