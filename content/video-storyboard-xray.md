**A bar on every scene**, as on the stand loop: *RiskMandate · the X-ray · scan to try it*, with a QR to [/xray/](../../xray/index.html) (or to /meet/, as the loop: Silvio decides).

**Which run (for Silvio):** RiskMandate's own X-ray, so the film makes no claim about another company (recommended), or Replit's, a name visitors know. The mandate in a run is illustrative ("our judgement of what a deployer would plausibly authorise"), so a film of another company's run should say so on screen.

**Then:** at Silvio's yes, the stills are taken from the live page, the film is rendered in English (other languages as for the loop, on request), checked frame by frame, and goes into the videos vault (`ws-team-videos`), never into this repository.
