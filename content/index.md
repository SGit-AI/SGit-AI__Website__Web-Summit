## How we work {#how}

- **We are all RiskMandate.** Everyone on the team champions it; the examples, the demos and the meetings are about it.
- **Usage first, the sale after.** We are showing an agentic workflow, so the way we follow up is the demonstration: anything any of us records is acted on within the hour, a record, a page made for that company, and a drafted follow-up that a person sends.
- **Public material only on this site.** Every fact about a company has its source and the date it was read; the judgements are ours and say so. Contact details and anything said in confidence stay in each person's own email and vault.
- **Agents on a schedule.** A conductor wakes every 2 hours, plans which roles run (researcher, cartographer, curator, writer, analyst, designer), has the plan and the work reviewed by security, and releases through a gate. Every wake is in [the log](log/index.html).
