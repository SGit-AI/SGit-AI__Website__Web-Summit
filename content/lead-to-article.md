## The six steps {#steps}

1. **Talk.** Have the conversation. If it fits, show them the live demo: our agents at work, then a private page about their own agents' reach, mandate and gap (Vicky's proposal, about 5 to 8 minutes).
2. **Ask for a yes, out loud.** Two separate questions:
   - *"Can we follow up with you?"* If yes, take the contact they prefer.
   - *"Can we write about this on PromptUGAL?"* A spoken yes is enough for a short mention or quote. A full interview needs their signed approval of the draft, through the newsroom's interview steps.
3. **Record a 30-second memo**, straight after, to your own agent (the template is below). Company and role, what they said, what they asked for, and which yes they gave.
4. **Same evening, your agent drafts two things:**
   - **The follow-up, private.** It goes into the company's thread in the private CRM, never into this repository or any page (the comms protocol).
   - **An article draft, only if they said yes.** It goes to the newsroom (newsroom@web-summit.dinis), marked *"from a conversation on <date>, yes given"*. The newsroom checks it and publishes. Without a yes, nothing about them is written. Their company page keeps public facts only.
5. **Hot lead?** If they want pricing, a meeting or more, post them in the team's leads group that day. Nimay or Dinis follows up within a day (WS-29).
6. **Next morning:** you read and send the follow-up yourself. Agents never send. The newsroom publishes approved articles. Anyone can ask for a correction at any time.

## What stays private, and what can be public {#privacy}

| Stays private (the CRM, never here) | Can be public (PromptUGAL, the site) |
|---|---|
| The person's name, unless they agreed to be named | What their company publishes about itself |
| Contact details, always | What they said, if they said yes to being written about |
| Anything said in confidence | A quote, exactly as they agreed it |
| The follow-up and the conversation itself | That we met, if they agreed |

## The memo template {#memo}

Say this into your phone, to your own agent, in about 30 seconds:

> *"Conversation memo. Company: <company>. Role: <their role, not their name>. Where: <the stand, a session, an event>. What they told us about their agents or their flows: <...>. What they asked for: <...>. Follow-up yes: <yes or no>. Write-about yes: <none / short mention / full interview>. Hot lead: <yes or no>."*
