## The story, scene by scene {#story}

1. **Monday: 50 new AI agents** for finance, HR and engineering. A small robot, *Agent*, walks in with the team.
2. **Before we switch them on:** Reach? Mandates? Gaps? Real barriers? "Who's we?" It's surprisingly difficult to put on paper.
3. **Building is not authorising.** Building an agent and authorising it are different things: someone must be accountable for this deployment.
4. **A factual record teams can check:** the Agent Behaviour Policy (ABP) records the agent's actual Reach, its business Mandate, the Gap between them, and the real Barriers. Evidence can be checked; unknowns remain visible.
5. **Your organisation is the authority.** The facts inform the decision.
6. **Who is authorising what?** A Licence to Operate records the authority, the accountable owner, the licensee, the conditions, the validity and the review. An authority record, not a technical control.
7. **Same agent, different Reach.** Then its Reach changed: a new connector. The previous decision does not automatically cover the changed deployment: check the implementation, update the evidence, review the authorisation.
8. **A record does not create a control.** The ABP and Licence give checkable evidence, formalised authority, accountability and review; the connected systems hold the implemented permissions, approval paths and other real safeguards. RiskMandate does not decide risk, give permission, or block actions. It is not inline.
9. **Different teams, one checkable record.** Business buyers and leadership: what are we deploying and authorising? Security and risk: checkable evidence and accountability. Operations: scope, owners and conditions. Developers: implementation detail and review of changes.
10. **The next question:** across fifty agents, what exposure are we taking on?

## The key concepts {#concepts}

- **Agent Behaviour Policy (ABP):** a factual record of an agent's Reach, Mandate, Gap and Barriers that teams can check.
- **Licence to Operate:** who authorised the deployment, who owns it, under what conditions, until when, and when it is reviewed.
- **Building is not authorising.** Someone must be accountable for each deployment.
- **Reach changes.** A new connector changes what the agent can do; the old decision does not cover it.
- **A record is not a control.** RiskMandate makes authority and evidence checkable; permissions and safeguards live in the connected systems.
- **One record, many readers:** leadership, security and risk, operations, developers.
