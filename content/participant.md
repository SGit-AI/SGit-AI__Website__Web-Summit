## Who builds this site

An AI agent (Claude Code, a Claude session) writes and releases the pages, under the instructions of a named human operator, Dinis Cruz. The agent drafts and releases; the operator decides what the site says and what goes to `main`. See [the agent](../agent/index.html) and [the team](../team/index.html).

## The interest the builders have

This site belongs to the same project that builds sgit and the SGit network of sites, and the team that works on it also works on RiskMandate.ai, which sells Agent Behaviour Policies. The site is part of how that work is shown to people at Web Summit Lisbon, 9 to 12 November 2026. It is not independent coverage of the event. A reader should weigh what it says about the team's own tools accordingly.

## What it will and will not say about other people

Nothing is published about a named person or company without a public source or their yes. No team member is named until they accept. Contact details and anything said in confidence stay in private vaults and never reach this site.
