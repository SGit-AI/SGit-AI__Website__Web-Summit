**We are looking for investors for a team that is already in place.** A small group of people, each with their own team of AI agents, building and running real products in public: this site, the newsroom [PrompTugal.news](https://promptugal.news/), and [RiskMandate](https://riskmandate.ai/), the way to give an AI agent a mandate and see the gap between what it can do and what it was asked to do. Everything is open source. What we sell is the execution: services, and agentic teams customised for a person, a team or a company. The company people can invest in is **My Feeds** (registered as MyFeeds AI, Unipessoal, Lda), in Loulé, in the Algarve.

**There are no numbers on these pages, and no projections, on purpose.** Dinis Cruz, 6 November 2026: *"we're not going to make any projections based on revenue because we don't know."* The first six months to a year are for discovering the market, selling services and scaling the agentic team. What we can show instead is the work, which is all public, and how fast it moves.

## How to read these pages {#read}

- **Every claim has its source**, and most sources are Dinis's own writing on [newsroom.sgit.ai](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/), which says plainly that agents write its articles from his voice memos. Outside coverage is thin; we say so rather than lean on it. The research behind every page, with what we could not confirm, is [public](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/team/investors/research.md).
- **What runs is said to run; what is designed is said to be designed.** This site's rule: a thing not in [REALITY.md](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/blob/dev/REALITY.md) does not exist.
- **These pages change as we get traction.** Each change is a commit, so the history of the pitch is [in the open too](https://github.com/SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit/commits/dev/content/investors).
- **No contact details on any page.** If someone sent you here, reply to them; that is how every conversation with us works ([the comms protocol](../comms-protocol/index.html)).
