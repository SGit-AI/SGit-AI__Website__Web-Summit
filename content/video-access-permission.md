By Silvio Switha, 5 November 2026. 30 seconds, subtitled, made to play without sound: one of the short films that run in a loop on the screen at our stand on Thursday 12 November, next to the other short films, and a first post for LinkedIn (WS-126). Its two lines are the ones on the T-shirts: *Your AI has access. Does it have permission?* and *AI Agents. Your Rules.*

## What it shows {#what}

1. **Meet your AI agent.** A business owner asks it to check the company's finances.
2. **It can access your files.** Access granted: it opens the company's finances and analyses the numbers.
3. **Share outside your business?** It tries to send the report out of the company. Pending review.
4. **The question:** you gave it access. Did you give it permission?
5. **RiskMandate. AI Agents. Your Rules.**

## The key concepts {#concepts}

- **Access** is what the agent can reach: the files, the finances, the email.
- **Permission** is what the business authorised it to do: check the numbers, not send them out.
- **The gap** between the two is where the risk lives. RiskMandate writes down, for each agent, its reach, its mandate and the gap: *Know their reach. Define their mandate. Control the gap.*
