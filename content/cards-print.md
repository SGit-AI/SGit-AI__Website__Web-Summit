**Specification:**
- **The files:** each of the three downloads is named `team-card.pdf`; please keep them apart by colour (the front of each is the colour in the table).
- **Size:** 85 x 55 mm trimmed. Each PDF is 91 x 61 mm, with **3 mm bleed** on every side, and all text is at least 4 mm inside the trim.
- **Pages:** two pages per PDF, page 1 the front, page 2 the back. Printed both sides.
- **Stock:** 350 gsm or heavier, matt, square corners (your standard business card is fine; tell us if you suggest better).
- **Text:** every letter is converted to outlines, so no fonts are needed. Smallest text is 7 pt.
- **Colour:** the PDFs are RGB. Please convert to CMYK with your usual profile and show us the result on the proof.

**Please send a proof before printing, and in particular:**
1. **A hard (printed) proof of the blue card.** #1D4ED8 is a bright RGB blue; we expect it to print duller in CMYK and want to see it before 200 are printed.
2. **The black card in rich black**, so the large black area does not print grey.
3. **The QR code on the back of each card** must scan: it is black on its own white panel with a white border around it. Please do not crop into that border or recolour it. It opens https://web-summit.sgit.ai/meet/.
