Drafted by video@web-summit.silvio on 5 November 2026 (WS-75), from [the list of next videos](../index.html#next), in the shape of [the loop's storyboard](../storyboard-loop/index.html). Every line uses words already on this site; a new claim about RiskMandate goes to Dinis first. **For:** companies that build and sell agents. **Where:** LinkedIn and YouTube, and the stand's laptop. **Length:** about 60 seconds, captioned; the mirror of [the buyers' storyboard](../storyboard-buyers/index.html).

| # | Time | Picture | On-screen text | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00-0:07 | A vendor demoing its robot to a customer; the demo goes well | **Your customer loves the demo.** | stop |
| 2 | 0:07-0:14 | The customer's security lead raises a hand | **Then security asks: what can your agent reach?** | stop |
| 3 | 0:14-0:22 | The vendor freezes; a long list of systems scrolls past | **It's surprisingly difficult to put on paper.** | explain |
| 4 | 0:22-0:34 | The vendor hands over a card: Reach, Mandate, Gap, Barriers; the security lead reads it | **An Agent Behaviour Policy:** a factual record of its Reach, Mandate, Gap and Barriers. Evidence can be checked; unknowns stay visible. | explain |
| 5 | 0:34-0:44 | The card passes along the customer's table: leadership, security, operations, developers | **Different teams. One checkable record.** | explain |
| 6 | 0:44-0:52 | A new connector plugs in; the card updates its version | **Then its Reach changed:** update the evidence, review the authorisation. | explain |
| 7 | 0:52-1:00 | The robot looks at the camera; logo, QR | **Know their reach. Define their mandate. Control the gap.** | convert |

**What it must not say:** that the record makes the agent safe. The ABP and the Licence give checkable evidence and formalised authority; the connected systems hold the permissions and safeguards ([fifty new agents](../fifty-new-agents/index.html#concepts)).

**Open:** scene 6 follows [fifty new agents](../fifty-new-agents/index.html#story) (scene 7: check the implementation, update the evidence, review the authorisation); keep it, or end on scene 5?
