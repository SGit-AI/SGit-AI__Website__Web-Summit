# The company map

The companies at Web Summit 2026 that matter to us, from public material, with a proposed decision and an owner for each.

Site version: v0.1.14

- [1Password](1password/index.md) (P1, meet, confirmed, owner to decide): Secures every identity, from people to AI agents: passwords, passkeys and secrets for over 150,000 businesses, and a security layer for how agents authenticate and connect to the internet. Why: partner/story: building "the security layer for how agents authenticate and connect"; agent identity and secrets sit next to sgit vaults and agent policies
- [Cisco (Outshift by Cisco)](cisco-outshift/index.md) (P1, meet, confirmed, owner to decide): Networking and security; at Web Summit it presents Outshift by Cisco (incubation of emerging tech) and Cisco DevNet (developer platform for agentic AI), with open-source projects in agentic AI, quantum and next-gen infrastructure. Why: partner/story: Outshift by Cisco and DevNet position themselves as the developer platform for agentic AI with open-source projects; a route to the agent-infrastructure community
- [Cloudflare](cloudflare/index.md) (P1, meet, confirmed, owner to decide): Connectivity cloud and security platform; its homepage now leads with "Build for the agent era". Why: partner/story: co-founder and CEO is a featured 2026 speaker and the site now says "build for the agent era"; edge platform for agents
- [CrowdStrike](crowdstrike/index.md) (P1, write-about, confirmed, owner to decide): Global cybersecurity company with a cloud-native, AI-native platform protecting endpoints, cloud workloads, identities and data. Why: story: co-founder and CEO is a featured 2026 speaker; AI-native endpoint, identity and cloud security, the reference point for any agent-security story
- [Delinea](delinea/index.md) (P1, meet, confirmed, owner to decide): Identity security platform that governs what humans, machines and AI agents do once they have access, extending privileged access management into continuous, just-in-time authorization across on-premises, multi-cloud and ephemeral infrastructure. Why: partner/story: governs what humans, machines and AI agents do once they have access; just-in-time authorization is the identity-side twin of RiskMandate/SHarness policy enforcement
- [Hexamind](hexamind/index.md) (P1, meet, unconfirmed, owner to decide): French AI company ("L'IA utile et maîtrisée"); publishes Hexgate, an open-source (MIT) authorization layer for AI agents that gates every tool call through allow/deny/approval decisions by role, with signed policy bundles and an audit trail. Why: partner/competitor: Hexgate enforces company policy on each agent tool call, the nearest open-source neighbour to RiskMandate and SHarness; compare designs, possible joint demo
- [KPMG](kpmg-portugal/index.md) (P1, meet, confirmed, owner to decide): Global network of professional services firms providing audit, tax and advisory services; listed for Web Summit 2026 as KPMG Portugal. Why: customer/partner: KPMG Portugal is an event partner and runs the Global Tech Innovator final in Lisbon; audit and advisory firms are the buyers and validators of AI governance and board-level cyber advice
- [Mastra](mastra/index.md) (P1, meet, confirmed, owner to decide): Open-source TypeScript framework and enterprise platform for building, iterating and managing production-grade agents. Why: partner/customer: leading open-source TypeScript agent framework; agents built on it need behaviour policies and shared encrypted state (RiskMandate, sgit)
- [Polygraf AI](polygraf-ai/index.md) (P1, meet, confirmed, owner to decide): AI security and governance for mission-critical operations: its AI Behavioral Control Plane gives real-time visibility, governance and inline enforcement across human and agentic AI interactions, to stop data exposure, unauthorised AI actions and compliance violations. Why: customer/partner: an AI Behavioral Control Plane enforcing inline governance on human and agentic AI interactions is the closest commercial neighbour to RiskMandate Agent Behaviour Policies; compare notes, possible integration
- [Primitive](primitive/index.md) (P1, meet, confirmed, owner to decide): Email built for agents: gives AI agents their own email address and the tools to act on it over standard email protocols; backed by Y Combinator. Why: partner/story: "email built for agents" gives each agent its own address; directly relevant to our email-agent brief (one official email agent per person, signed mail)
- [Qase](qase/index.md) (P1, meet, confirmed, owner to decide): Test management platform used by 2,000+ engineering teams; launching Tiden, which records what was asked and checks every change made by coding agents against it. Why: partner/story: its new product Tiden keeps a record of what you asked for and checks every agent-written change against it, the same idea as our gate and briefs
- [Replit](replit/index.md) (P1, meet, confirmed, owner to decide): Agentic software creation platform that lets anyone build applications in natural language; millions of users and 500,000+ business users. Why: story/partner: agentic software creation for non-developers, and a featured speaker (Michele Catasta); the "everyone codes with agents" audience for SHarness and RiskMandate
- [Startup Portugal](startup-portugal/index.md) (P1, meet, confirmed, owner to decide): Portugal's national startup agency; with Web Summit runs Road 2 Web Summit, selecting up to 125 Portuguese startups for the 2026 edition with a bootcamp in Porto and Lisbon and a Portugal stand.
