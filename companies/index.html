The company map

Every company we think is going to Web Summit 2026 and that matters to us: who they are, what they do, why it matters, and what we propose to do about it. Built from public material only, each fact with its source and the date read; the judgements are ours, and each decision is a proposal until the company's owner on our team takes it. Owners are the operator's decision.

Read the team brief and plan (newsroom.sgit.ai) and sgit.ai/llms.txt for who we are (sgit vaults, RiskMandate Agent Behaviour Policies, SHarness, the newsroom API). Then read the server-rendered HTML of websummit.com (robots.txt allows all but /api/): the 2026 event partners page (first 40 partners embedded in the page), the featured startups page (first 100, alphabetical A to Su, all GROWTH), the speakers and featured attendees pages (first 20 each), the schedule page (first sessions), and the track pages. Each entry carries conferenceSlug "lis26", which is what makes it a 2026 confirmation; each links to a public /appearances/lis26/<id>/<slug> or /attendees/lis26/<id>/<slug> page, from which the company website was read. The rest of each list is loaded client-side from a search service and was NOT read (see notes). Added press (Lusa via AMAN, Fast Company ME, The Portugal Guides, Executive Digest, IBTimes Colombia) and one third-party startup list (Future Feed), used only as "unconfirmed" leads unless the company is also on websummit.com. Filtered for relevance to us: AI-agent security and governance, identity, devtools for agents, GRC, Portugal, and a few investors and policy bodies. Homepage titles/meta descriptions were read for speakers' companies. First pass by the researcher on 1 November 2026: the first page of each official list only (partners, featured startups to 'Su', speakers, featured attendees); the rest of each list is loaded by the browser and was not read.
