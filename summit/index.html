Web Summit 2026

What is public about Web Summit 2026 and what matters to us in it: the facts, the programme, the side events in Lisbon and the Algarve, and the news. Kept by the curator from public sources, each item linked; where something is unconfirmed, it says so. Read on 2026-11-01.

The facts

nameWeb Summit 2026 (Lisbon); Web Summit's own conference code is lis26 source
dates9 to 12 November 2026 source
opening nightMonday 9 November 2026: 'Opening Night' on Centre Stage from 18:00 (the title is still listed as 'Coming soon...'), then Night Summit 20:00-23:00 in Pavilion 5 (FIL), with regional meet-and-greets source
conference daysTuesday 10, Wednesday 11 and Thursday 12 November 2026. The published schedule runs from about 10:00 to about 17:40 local time (Lisbon is on WET, UTC+0, in November) source
venueMEO Arena and FIL, Rossio dos Olivais, 1990-231 Lisbon, Parque das Nações. The site's config still also gives the arena's old name, 'Altice Arena' source
attendance claimed 202670,000+ attendees (Web Summit's meetups and creator-programme pages). Over 15,000 companies a year, 10,000 of them startups, and over 2,500 exhibiting (Web Summit's evergreen 'what to expect' and startup-journey copy). Paddy Cosgrave told Tek Notícias that exhibition space is sold out, with more than 3,000 companies and 30,000 m2 of temporary halls. These are claims, not audited figures source
attendance 2025 reference2025 edition: 71,386 attendees from 157 countries, 1,857 investors from 86 countries, 2,725 startups from 108 countries, 869 speakers (Web Summit's own figures) source
speakersThird parties report 850+ speakers. Web Summit's speakers page shows a first wave weighted towards Chinese AI and robotics companies (AgiBot, Manus AI, Unitree, Galbot, Replit, ModelBest, Xiaomi, Pony.ai, MiniMax, Tencent) source
tracks17 content tracks plus Centre Stage. The track pages are: AI Summit, Commerce Summit, Corporate Innovation Summit, Creative Summit, Cybersecurity Summit, Developer Summit, Energy Summit, Future of Work Summit, Growth Summit, Health Summit, Marketing Summit, MoneyConf, New Media Summit, Policy Summit, SaaS Summit, Sport Summit and Venture Summit. The schedule also filters by Masterclasses, Investor Masterclass, Startup Masterclass, Meetup, Night Summit, Press conference and Women in tech source
stagesCentre Stage (MEO Arena) and Stages 2-14 (for example Stage 8 = Cybersecurity Summit and MoneyConf, Stage 10 = Developer Summit and Energy, Stage 11 = Policy Summit, Stage 13 = AI Summit), Masterclass 1-5, Meetup 1-6, Developer Lounge, Community Space, Pavilion 5 (Night Summit) source
schedule statusLive and still being added to ('We're adding more every week'). 823 sessions were published when read on 2026-11-01: 20 on 9 Nov, 280 on 10 Nov, 303 on 11 Nov and 220 on 12 Nov source
ticketsGeneral attendee: the page shows €1,595 and €1,297 excl. sales tax (probably the full price and the current offer). VIP: €1,950 and €1,585. Chairperson: €24,950 and €20,285. Investor: €0 (by application). Platinum Investor: €1,950. The registration FAQ prices a replacement ticket at €1,595 source
appThe Web Summit app (App Store and Google Play) holds your ticket QR code (needed to register), profile, chat, personal schedule, meetings, venue map, perks and jobs, and connection export source
night summitThis year Night Summit is on the show floor, in Pavilion 5 (FIL): Mon 9 Nov 20:00-23:00, Tue 10 Nov 18:00-21:00, Wed 11 Nov 18:00-21:00. Speed networking from 18:00, 100+ meetups, games. Accredited attendees only. Last entry 21:30, and after 17:00 the way in is the North Entrance near Moscavide metro source
women in techWomen in Tech tickets are sold out. Web Summit says 40% of startups at the 2025 edition were women-founded. The 7th annual Gender in Tech report was published on 27 Oct 2026 source
startup programmeThe 2026 startup programme is sold out (applications for 2027 are open). Accepted startups get 3 tickets, investor meetings (15-minute pre-scheduled slots), and can apply for PITCH (105 contenders), Startup Showcase and Mentor Hours (45-minute group sessions). The ALPHA, BETA and GROWTH package names do not appear on the 2026 pages read; the schedule has a 'GROWTH Lounge' location source
investor programmeInvestor ticket (free, by application): startup discovery tools, investor meeting area, Investor Masterclasses. Platinum Investor €1,950: adds invite-only side events, the Investor Lounge and fast track. 'Venture' is the investor gathering. There is also a Limited Partner (LP) offering source
developer programmeFree tickets for active open-source contributors, chosen by an AI review of applicants. The 2026 programme is sold out. There is a Web Summit Hackathon in the Developer Lounge: pre-hackathon meetup 10 Nov 12:30, hackathon 11 Nov, winners announced 12 Nov 13:45 source
pressMedia accreditation by application at /media/. Web Summit lists media attendees including CNBC, Bloomberg Media, NPR, The Atlantic, WSJ, NYT, Reuters and The Guardian. Press releases, photos and videos are linked from the media page. The press-release list itself sits in a Google Sheet that is not public source
badge pickupCollect your lanyard and wristband at Humberto Delgado Airport Terminal 1 (Sat 7 Nov 09:30-22:00; Sun 8 and Mon 9 Nov 07:30-22:00; Tue 10 Nov 07:30-15:00) or at Pavilhão de Portugal, Alameda dos Oceanos (Sat 7 and Sun 8 Nov 12:00-18:00; Mon 9 Nov 12:00-22:00; Tue 10 and Wed 11 Nov 07:30-17:00; Thu 12 Nov 07:30-16:45). Bring your ticket QR (in the app) and photo ID. Replacing one lost item costs €100. Times are subject to change source
getting thereOriente metro station (red line) is about 300 m from MEO Arena and 600 m from Pavilhão de Portugal. From Baixa-Chiado, take the green line to Alameda and then the red line, or the blue line to São Sebastião and then the red line. Carris buses 705, 725, 728, 750, 759 and 794 stop at Gare do Oriente. There are taxi and ride-share drop-off points. Hotels are booked through the partner b-network. November weather is 12-18°C with rain possible, and many areas are outdoors source
opening hoursNo overall venue opening hours are published. Known times: registration hours (above), sessions from about 10:00, Night Summit hours (above). Event-level listings give 09:00-18:30 (Algarve Evolution's listing; not official) source
wifiOfficial event wifi is free throughout MEO Arena source
lisbon funding contextOn 23 Sep 2026 Lisbon City Council approved €8M for Web Summit (more may follow), and is studying keeping the event until 2038 source
