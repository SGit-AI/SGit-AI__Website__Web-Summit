# The stand loop (WS-72): what was made

By video@web-summit.silvio for Silvio Switha, 5 to 7 November 2026. For the iMac at our stand on Thursday 12 November.

The first plan here (a 2-minute cut from Silvio's two films) is withdrawn: on 7 November both films were found to carry errors (see [PUBLISH.md](PUBLISH.md)). The loop was made instead from Silvio's own pictures:

- **Eight slides, 67 seconds, silent**, in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Chinese and Hindi, **one video per language** (Silvio, 8 November: separate, not six in a row; that video was removed). The question, Reach, Mandate, the gap, one agent to 1,000+, "Building is not authorising", one checkable record for every team, and the close with a QR to https://web-summit.sgit.ai/meet/.
- **Every line is already on this site**; nothing new is claimed about RiskMandate.
- **Checked slide by slide in all six languages**, and by a security sub-agent; two source pictures retouched (tiny garbled labels, a faded label), one with a typo painted out, one with invented figures blurred.
- **The stand page, to choose a language:** `riskmandate-stand-loop.html`: the slides loop in English with a button for each language (EN, PT, ES, FR, 中文, हिन्दी) always on screen; a visitor taps one and sees the whole loop in it, then it returns to English. One self-contained file, works offline.
- **Quality:** each slide photographed at 3840x2160 and rendered to 1920x1080 at about 3.5 Mbit/s (8 November); the first versions were photographed at 1920 and enlarged for the zoom, which softened the text.
- **Where it is:** the vault `ws-team-videos` (h9in5em4), `stand-loop/`: the six MP4s, the stand page, a preview per language, the clean pictures, and `build/` (the scripts that make it). Every file is under 30 MB.
- **On the iMac:** open the stand page in Chrome and press F (full screen), so visitors can choose; or play one language's MP4 on repeat in QuickTime or VLC.
- **Sound:** none, on purpose (a noisy hall; every slide carries its own text). Music only with a licence for commercial use, supplied by Silvio.

Open: a native speaker of each language checks the translations before Thursday (Chinese and Hindi first); the QR moves to a contact page once Dinis decides where visitors' contacts go.
