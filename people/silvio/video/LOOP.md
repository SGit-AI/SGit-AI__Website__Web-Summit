# The stand loop (WS-72): what was made

By video@web-summit.silvio for Silvio Switha, 5 to 7 November 2026. For the iMac at our stand on Thursday 12 November.

The first plan here (a 2-minute cut from Silvio's two films) is withdrawn: on 7 November both films were found to carry errors (see [PUBLISH.md](PUBLISH.md)). The loop was made instead from Silvio's own pictures:

- **Eight slides, 67 seconds, silent**, in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Chinese and Hindi, one video each, and one video with the six in a row (6 min 42 s). The question, Reach, Mandate, the gap, one agent to 1,000+, "Building is not authorising", one checkable record for every team, and the close with a QR to https://web-summit.sgit.ai/meet/.
- **Every line is already on this site**; nothing new is claimed about RiskMandate.
- **Checked slide by slide in all six languages**, and by a security sub-agent; two source pictures retouched (tiny garbled labels, a faded label), one with a typo painted out, one with invented figures blurred.
- **Where it is:** the vault `ws-team-videos` (h9in5em4), `stand-loop/`: the MP4s, the same slides as self-contained HTML pages, a preview per language, the clean pictures, and `build/` (the scripts that make it). The six-language video (117 MB) is uploaded by Silvio with sgit.
- **On the iMac:** play the English MP4 (or the six-language one) on repeat in QuickTime or VLC; or open the HTML page in Chrome and press F.
- **Sound:** none, on purpose (a noisy hall; every slide carries its own text). Music only with a licence for commercial use, supplied by Silvio.

Open: a native speaker of each language checks the translations before Thursday (Chinese and Hindi first); the QR moves to a contact page once Dinis decides where visitors' contacts go.
