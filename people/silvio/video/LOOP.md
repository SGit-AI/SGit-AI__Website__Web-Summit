## The rules for every frame

- No sound needed; every scene carries its own caption and the RiskMandate name.
- Large text, readable from three metres: at most two lines and about eight words a line, centred in the top two thirds (the bar holds the bottom).
- No scene longer than 20 seconds; the last frame is the invitation, then it loops.
- **The bar**, always on, along the bottom, with the QR to `https://web-summit.sgit.ai/meet/` at its right:
  - EN: **RiskMandate · Agent Behaviour Policies for AI agents · scan to book a demo**
  - PT: **RiskMandate · Políticas de comportamento para agentes de IA · leia o QR para marcar uma demo**
