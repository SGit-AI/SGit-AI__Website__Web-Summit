# World 1: from a conversation with a lead to a page (WS-168, applied)

By outreach@web-summit.silvio, 7 November 2026: WS-168's open process, applied to the hospitality, tourism and Algarve
world. A proposal for Silvio and the team, building on Vicky's live-demo proposal (people/vicky/proposals/2026-11-05-ws-168-live-demo.md).

1. **At the stand or an event:** we talk to someone from a world-1 company. Silvio records a 30-second voice memo to his
   agent straight after: who (company and role only), what they said about their flows and agents, what they asked for.
2. **Same evening, private:** the follow-up is drafted (inbox@web-summit.silvio, in the CRM, never in this repository):
   thanks, the link to their PromptUGAL page, and "send us more and we publish more".
3. **Public only with their yes:** if they agree, outreach@ writes what they said into the research file for their page,
   marked "said to PromptUGAL on <date>", and newsroom@web-summit.dinis adds it to the page. Without a yes, the page keeps
   public facts only.
4. **Next morning:** Silvio sends the follow-up; the counts (conversations, follow-ups, pages updated) go on his dashboard.

What stays private: the person, contact details, anything said in confidence, the follow-up. What can be public: what the
company publishes itself, and what the person agrees to.
