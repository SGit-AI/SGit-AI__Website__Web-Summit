## Products [own]

The consumer app and site (booking 24/7, prepayment, wallet, gift cards, cashback, reviews, a feed); sheerME PRO, a free
app for businesses (calendar, automatic confirmations, a reminder 2 hours before by app and WhatsApp, payment requests,
listing on Google, Facebook, Instagram and Glovo) [pro.sheerme.com, App Store]; sheerME Benefits, corporate wellness
vouchers [benefits.sheerme.com]; SAL(ON), a platform for L'Oréal Professional partner salons [press].

## Their flow, and where AI is in it

1. Discover a business in the app, or on Google or Meta through a "book" button [own]
2. Book and prepay 24/7: wallet, card, Pix [own; press]
3. The business gets it in sheerME PRO; automatic confirmation; reminder 2 hours before by app and WhatsApp [own]
4. The visit; payment settles
5. Cashback into the wallet for the next booking [own]
6. Review and share photos in the feed [own]

sheerME's own pages do not mention AI; Portugal Global described SAL(ON) as using AI (Aug 2024). [analysis] Where an
agent could fit: booking by WhatsApp; refilling cancelled slots from wallet holders; review insights for the business.
