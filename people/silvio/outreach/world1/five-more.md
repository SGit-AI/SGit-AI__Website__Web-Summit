# World 1: the five companies Silvio added (7 November 2026)

Research for PromptUGAL pages, read on 7 November 2026 by a research agent of outreach@web-summit.silvio. None has a
PromptUGAL page yet. Tags as in README.md. All five are in Web Summit's "Travel & hospitality" industry; the four startups
publish no booth day or session. **Not published here, unconfirmed:** WeRoad's 2025 revenue and an August 2026 round;
Cadae's "~25% savings" (aggregator only).

## Lena Concierge (Switzerland), ALPHA

- Headline: "The AI concierge for alpine hospitality." "Lena answers guests around the clock in their own language,
  checks live availability in the property's PMS and takes the booking, inside the same chat. Live in Saas-Fee since
  summer 2026." [own: lenaconcierge.com]
- Products: guest chat in five languages; apaleo integration (offers, reservations, cancellation); Booking.com Demand
  when the property is full; restaurant requests by WhatsApp; an Owner App in beta. From CHF 89 a month per property.
  [own]
- Numbers, "measured 4 October 2026": 962 guest conversations in production; 35 properties connected. "Lena Concierge AG
  (in formation)". No founders named. [own]
- Flow: guest asks in the chat → answer from checked facts ("Prices and opening hours come from checked facts; the model
  chooses the fact and understands the question") → live availability and booking in the PMS → restaurant request by
  WhatsApp → emergencies "answered from fixed, tested text… no model writes that reply", and the owner is notified →
  the host sees questions and escalations in the Owner App. [own]
- Portugal: no link found. What we could publish: their "facts, not guesses" design as a pattern for a safe AI concierge,
  for hosts in Madeira, Serra da Estrela and the Algarve.
- Sources: <https://lenaconcierge.com/?lang=en>,
  <https://websummit.com/appearances/lis26/ea87aa28-83d6-42ca-bad5-baf2491d379a/lena-concierge-/>

## Cadae AI (United Kingdom), ALPHA

- Headline: "A virtual chief engineer for every hotel." "See where energy and water costs can come down. Cadae's AI
  prepares the next steps for your team to review and approve." [own: cadae.ai]
- Products: Performance Hotel Insights for energy and water, equipment-level monitoring, a portfolio dashboard, and an
  MCP server ("Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor to your Portfolio", scoped to the access you hold; 15 August 2026). Works
  with "a small group of founding hotel partners". Cadae Limited, London. [own]
- People: Kevin Bird, Co-Founder & CEO; Cedric Rodrigues, Co-Founder & CPO; Mark Brighton, Co-Founder & CFO; Alex
  Vakhitov, CTO. [own: about page]
- Flow: describe the hotel → build the performance picture (utility use, activity, weather) → investigate and cost the
  opportunities → agree and deliver the work → check outcomes against a baseline. Their rule: "Cadae does not commit
  budget, contact contractors or change how plant runs." [own: services; update of 5 Nov 2026]
- Portugal: no link found. What we could publish: hotel energy and water costs in Portugal, where water is scarce in the
  Algarve, with their split between proposed and verified savings as a buyer's checklist.
- Sources: <https://cadae.ai/en-GB>, <https://cadae.ai/en-GB/about>, <https://cadae.ai/en-GB/services>,
  <https://cadae.ai/en-GB/updates/virtual-chief-engineer>, <https://cadae.ai/en-GB/mcp>,
  <https://websummit.com/appearances/lis26/994635a7-943f-4d65-9da3-f6ac75d746f0/cadae-ai/>

## 241.travel (Austria), ALPHA

- Headline: "Spontan verreisen. Nächte geschenkt." (Travel on a whim. Nights free.) A members-only last-minute hotel club
  in Europe: every deal includes at least one free night (1+1 up to 6+1); arrivals from today to 20 days ahead; no
  prepayment. [own: 241.travel]
- Hotels pay 8.88% commission "erst nach Anreise des Gastes" (only after the guest arrives). 510 partner hotels; 13 team
  members. Operator: T.B.G Ellmau OG, Tyrol. [own]
- People: Samuel Hirschhuber, CEO and founder; Chiara Strauss, CFO and co-founder (and others on /ueber-uns). [own]
- AI: the concierge "Rio" answers messages (Anthropic or Google Gemini; voice by ElevenLabs). Their AI statement (July
  2026): "überall, wo es um dein Geld, deine Daten oder heikle Fälle geht, entscheidet bei uns ein Mensch" (wherever
  money, data or sensitive cases are involved, a person decides). Their Web Summit pitch adds "agentic systems for
  backoffice". [own; WS]
- Portugal: no link found. What we could publish: the 1+1 model explained for Portuguese hotels with empty off-season
  rooms, with their AI statement as an example of transparency.
- Sources: <https://241.travel>, <https://241.travel/so-funktioniert-1-plus-1>, <https://241.travel/ueber-uns>,
  <https://241.travel/ki-statement>, <https://websummit.com/appearances/lis26/963c34a2-04cf-4641-ba8b-741a65fa0d64/241.travel>

  Haji-Ioannou." [own; WS pitch]
- People: Giorgos Karavokyris, Founder & CEO [press: Proto Thema, 12 Jun 2026].
