## What they do

- Their headline: "Run your entire service business in one place" ("clients. team. time. profit."). [own: alteg.io]
- "Online booking, CRM, payments, payroll, inventory and analytics, connected in one fast, reliable platform for salons,
  clinics, studios and every team that works by appointment." Positioned as an "anti-marketplace": "Your clients stay
  yours… 0% commission on your revenue". [own]
- Web Summit pitch: "Vertical System of Record (SoR). The layer AI agents are built on." [WS appearance]

## Products [own]

Online booking widget (name and phone only), scheduling, CRM, WhatsApp/SMS/email/push notifications, payments and POS
(Adyen, Stripe, Viva Wallet), payroll, inventory, analytics, loyalty, the Altegio.me client app, an open API with 100+
integrations and an app store. For AI: four free MCP servers and the Altegio Analytics Agent.

## Their flow, and where AI is in it

1. Book 24/7 from the business's site, Google, Instagram or messengers [own]
2. Optional prepayment, membership or gift card [own]
3. The appointment lands in the calendar [own]
4. Reminders by WhatsApp, SMS, email or push [own]
5. The visit goes into the client's history; stock written off per service [own]
6. Payroll by formula [own]
7. Reports across locations [own]

AI today, on Altegio's own pages: the Altegio Pro MCP connects Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or Gemini to appointments,
clients and schedules and "reads and changes data on request", with changes reviewed before saving; a read-only MCP, a
Knowledge MCP and an Integrations Hub MCP; the Altegio Analytics Agent, read-only, which "analyzes your business without
changing appointments"; third-party AI booking agents in its app store. Altegio's own caveat: AI is "a development
direction… not a claim that every planned capability is available today". [own: /llms.txt, /en/mcp/, app store]
