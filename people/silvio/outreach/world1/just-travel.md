## What they do

- Their headline: "A plataforma global onde sua agência cresce" ("Just Travel | Sua agência no digital"). [own:
  justtraveltour.com]
- Travel agencies, creators and businesses get a free branded online store with "+1 milhão de produtos turísticos"
  connected by API (park tickets, hotels, cruises, cars, insurance, events), with checkout, anti-fraud, a sales
  dashboard and an optional AI sales agent; Just Travel earns a commission on each sale. [own]
- Web Summit pitch mentions "white-label e-commerce, global travel inventory, payments, automation, and AI". [WS]

## Products and prices [own]

A white-label store, live "em até 24 horas" (own domain, no-code editor, PIX, boleto or card with 3-D Secure); a catalogue
with Disney, Universal and SeaWorld tickets, 700,000 hotels and 5,000 car rental options through 15 global APIs; Just
Academy training; TRAVIA, "IA, CRM e Omnicanal". The store is R$0 a month (commission only); the AI and CRM plan R$199 a
month (promotion, from R$399).

## Their flow, and where AI is in it [own home page]

1. The agency activates its store: logo, colours, domain; live within 24 hours
2. It promotes links and campaigns
3. A lead comes in: TRAVIA answers on WhatsApp, qualifies the lead, builds the itinerary, sends the quote and
   schedules follow-up (AI today)
4. Cart and checkout: hotel, tickets, transport and insurance in one cart, the agency's own margin; TRAVIA can close
   the sale (AI today, in part)
5. Fulfilment without retyping: confirmed with the supplier, voucher sent, commission released in the panel
6. After the sale: service, loyalty, cart recovery, campaigns

Their own claim for TRAVIA: "68% leads qualificados sem agente", 3-minute average response, 24/7. [own] [analysis]
Where else an agent could fit: rebooking after a flight change; watching supplier prices to protect the agency's margin.
