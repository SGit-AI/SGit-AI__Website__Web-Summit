## DIG-IN

1. Venue data is collected "via ML image recognition and menu analysis" ([dig-in.io](https://www.dig-in.io/)). **AI acts here.**
2. Records are validated: "AI-enhanced data validation with continuous venue monitoring" ([dig-in.io](https://www.dig-in.io/)). **AI acts here.**
3. Menus, prices and locations are tracked over time, updated monthly ([Web Summit](https://websummit.com/appearances/lis26/05980d1e-1823-4436-bfd7-bb9dbe52be76/dig-in)).
4. Each venue's digital footprint is scored ([dig-in.io](https://www.dig-in.io/)).
5. Clients get it through the On-Trade Dashboard, an API and reports ([dig-in.io/products](https://www.dig-in.io/products)).
6. Brands decide where to sell and check execution in venues ([DIG-IN at Web Summit 2026](https://www.dig-in.io/insights/dig-in-web-summit-lisbon-2026)).

At the summit (their own article, 4 Nov 2026): stand on **Tuesday 10 November, Pavilion 3, booth G1-30**; a pitch in the
GROWTH Startup Showcase on **Thursday 12 November**.

## sheerME

1. A client finds a business in the app, or on Google or Meta through a "book" button ([sheerME](https://blog.sheerme.com/sobre/)).
2. Books and prepays, 24/7: wallet, card or Pix ([sheerME](https://blog.sheerme.com/missao/)).
3. The business gets it in sheerME PRO: automatic confirmation, a reminder 2 hours before by app and WhatsApp ([sheerME PRO](https://pro.sheerme.com/)).
4. The visit; cashback goes into the wallet ([sheerME](https://sheerme.com/)).
5. Reviews and photos in the feed ([sheerME](https://blog.sheerme.com/sobre/)).

sheerME's own pages do not say AI acts in these steps. (Portugal Global described its L'Oréal platform, SAL(ON), as using
AI: [Portugal Global, Aug 2024](https://www.portugalglobal.pt/pt/noticias/2024/agosto/sheerme-e-loreal-lancam-plataforma-no-brasil).)

## Altegio

1. A client books 24/7 from the business's site, Google, Instagram or messengers ([Altegio](https://alteg.io/en/online-booking/)).
2. Optional prepayment, membership or gift card (same page).
3. The appointment lands in the calendar; reminders by WhatsApp, SMS, email or push ([Altegio](https://alteg.io/)).
4. The visit goes into the client's history; stock written off; payroll by formula; reports ([Altegio](https://alteg.io/)).

**AI acts here, on Altegio's own pages:** the Altegio Pro MCP connects Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or Gemini to appointments,
clients and schedules, and "reads and changes data on request", with changes reviewed before saving; a read-only MCP;
the Altegio Analytics Agent, which "analyzes your business without changing appointments"
([MCP](https://alteg.io/en/mcp/), [Analytics Agent](https://alteg.io/en/appstore/altegio_analytics_agent/)). Their own
caveat: AI is "a development direction… not a claim that every planned capability is available today" ([llms.txt](https://alteg.io/llms.txt)).

## Just Travel

1. An agency activates its white-label store, live within 24 hours ([Just Travel](https://www.justtraveltour.com/)).
2. A lead comes in: **TRAVIA answers on WhatsApp, qualifies the lead, builds the itinerary, sends the quote and schedules
   follow-up** (same page). **AI acts here.**
3. Cart and checkout: hotel, tickets, transport and insurance, with the agency's own margin; TRAVIA can close the sale
   (same page). **AI acts here, in part.**
4. Fulfilment: confirmed with the supplier, voucher sent, commission released in the panel (same page).
5. After the sale: service, loyalty, cart recovery (same page).

Their claim for TRAVIA: "68% leads qualificados sem agente", 3-minute average response (same page).
