## What they do

- Their headline: "The Global Food Services Industry runs on fragmented and limited data." Subhead: "We are
  consolidating data that is shaping the future of customer acquisition strategies. From hyper-local dashboards to
  pan-European market insights." [own: dig-in.io]
- A data set of restaurants, bars and cafés (menus, prices, location, digital presence and which brands are visible in
  each venue), kept up to date over time; bought by drinks and food brands, restaurant groups and others to find where
  to sell and check how their brands are placed. Also runs a consumer restaurant marketplace, dig-in.pt. [own]
- Web Summit pitch: "DIG-IN maps the physical hospitality economy. Our Data engine tracks millions of verified outlets
  across Europe and Brazil, enriched with menus, pricing, and location data, and tracked over time." [WS appearance]

## Products [own: dig-in.io, /products]

On-Trade Dashboard (brand presence across venues, competitors, execution on the ground); Data Solutions and an API for
"brands, government bodies, and restaurant marketplaces"; advertising on dig-in.pt; Restaurant Tech (websites,
delivery, POS integration); reports: "Michelin Portugal Digital Experience Audit" (March 2026, 53 restaurants), "The
Visibility Gap" (January 2026), a scan of 374,035 UK restaurants and pubs.

## Their flow, and where AI is in it

1. Collect venue data "via ML image recognition and menu analysis" [own] (AI today)
2. Validate: "AI-enhanced data validation with continuous venue monitoring" [own] (AI today)
3. Enrich and track menus, prices and location over time; updated monthly [WS, own]
4. Score each venue's digital footprint (Digital Experience Score) [own]
5. Deliver through the dashboard, the API and reports [own]
6. The client decides: distribution gaps, competitor presence, activation, checks in the venue [own]

[analysis] Where an agent could fit: a weekly "venues to visit" list per sales rep; questions in plain language over
the dashboard; alerts on menu and price changes.
