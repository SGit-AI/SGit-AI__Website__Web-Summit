## How a business trip moves (their 27 July release)

1. A traveller, or a company's own AI agent, asks in Claude, Teams or Google Chat
2. The request reaches Egencia AI through the connector (MCP)
3. An orchestration layer splits it into tasks
4. Specialised agents shop and book flights and hotels, applying the company's policy, the traveller's profile and
   negotiated rates
5. Bookings and receipts flow to Concur Expense
6. The trip appears in the company's reports

The release names no approval step before a booking: a good question for the session.
