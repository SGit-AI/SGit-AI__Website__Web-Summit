## AI, in their own news

- 9 October 2025: first agentic AI products. Smart Messenger "gathers relevant partner, property, and reservation
  information to autonomously take action" (for Extranet partners in English-speaking markets first); Auto-Reply
  (global); AI Trip Support, 24/7 in 10 markets, handing complex cases to a person; AI Voice Support (English, German);
  AI Rental Helper; Flight Search Summaries. [own: news.booking.com]
- 20 May 2026: a car-rental app inside ChatGPT; the booking is completed on Booking.com. [own]
- 23 September 2026: CEO Glenn Fogel: LLM traffic is "significantly below 1%" of room nights; building a booking agent
  "wouldn't be that hard", trust is the hard part. [press: Skift]
- September 2026: Lola, an AI agent "part of Booking Holdings" that plans and books hotels, restaurants and events; not a
  Booking.com feature. [own: lola.com; press: Android Headlines]

## How a booking moves, and where AI acts (their statements)

1. Search: Smart Search and Flight Search Summaries; off-site, AI assistants (the ChatGPT app, Google AI Mode)
2. Choose
3. Reserve and pay on Booking.com
4. Messages between property and guest: Smart Messenger and Auto-Reply act for the partner
5. Changes and cancellations: AI Trip Support and AI Voice Support, with a human handoff
6. Car-rental questions: AI Rental Helper
