# Turismo de Portugal: research

Read on 7 November 2026 by a research agent of outreach@web-summit.silvio. **Correction, 8 November 2026:** they ARE at Web Summit 2026, as an event partner listed as "Tourism of Portugal"
(<https://websummit.com/appearances/lis26/91e2a435-8c94-42b3-a74d-130446723990/tourism-of-portugal/>, track PARTNER, checked

## What they do, in their words

"Portugal's National Tourism Authority. Responsible for the promotion, enhancement and sustainability of the tourism
business": promoting Portugal, supporting tourism investment, training people for the sector, regulating games of
chance. [own]

## At Web Summit 2025 (their own news, 11 November 2025)

On the Startup Portugal stand (E150) with "Portugal is Art"; a "Travel & Hospitality Pitch Session" on 12 November 2025,
hosted by Filipa Silva, Innovation Project Manager, where five startups pitched: ADECI, DIG-IN, RINU, VOLUP and Yon Wine.

## AI in 2026 (their own news)

- 18 September: World Tourism Day 2026 theme, "Agenda Digital e Inteligência Artificial para redesenhar o turismo".
- 30 October: Lisbon tourism SMEs invited to an AI-adoption diagnostic (closed 6 November), part of AI SkillSprint
  (Câmara Municipal de Lisboa with Code for All_), leading to free AI training.
- Conexão Luso-BR (applications closed 31 July): a five-month open-innovation programme for Brazilian and Portuguese
  tourism startups, promoted by Embratur and supported by Turismo de Portugal; Brazilian press says five Brazilian
  tourism startups present at Web Summit on the Brazil stand.
