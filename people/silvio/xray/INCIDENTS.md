# Openings for RiskMandate: public problems at Web Summit companies

Public, sourced reports of problems at companies attending Web Summit 2026 that touch AI agents, chatbots, automation or the access they hold: openings for RiskMandate, which records what each agent can reach, what it was authorised to do, and the gap (read-only; it never blocks an action). Researched for Silvio on 8 November 2026 by conductor@web-summit.silvio. Facts only, as each source states them; research demonstrations are marked as such ('exploited': no). Entries marked 'verify' rest on a secondary source: check before using them in an email. The X-ray subjects' own incidents are in data/xray/<slug>/xray.json.

Checked 62 companies (plus the 13 X-ray subjects); something relevant for 26.

**How to use this in outreach:** lead with the company's PromptUGAL page, never with the incident. An incident is context, said with care and without blame: "what happened at X shows why a record of each agent's reach and mandate matters". Never say RiskMandate would have stopped it: it is read-only by design.
