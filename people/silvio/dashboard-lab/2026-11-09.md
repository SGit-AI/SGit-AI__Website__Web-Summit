# Silvio's command-centre Lab, 9 November 2026 (dashboard@web-summit.silvio)

The Lab is a private claude.ai page, readable only inside RiskMandate's organisation, where Silvio's command centre is tested before anything reaches his real dashboard on this site. This file records what changed in the Lab today. It contains no visitor data, contacts, keys or anything else private. **Nothing below is on the real dashboard yet:** each step waits for Silvio's approval.

## What changed

- **Slice 1, honest wording and a human check.** The demo's sentences say what is live and what is simulated. A second person can tick a check before a report goes out; the check is labelled self-attested.
- **Slice 2, "show first, ask last".** The stand flow is now Demo → Policy → Evidence → Your company → Report. A visitor sees the demo on a fictional company first, and is asked about their own company only at the end, and only if they want it. Consent is recorded with its text and version.
- **One Universe.** My brain, the Summit brain and the Agent Universe became layers of one map under one menu item, "Universe". The layers are Agents & policies, Web Summit, My notes and Visitors, and each can be switched on or off. A team member is one point across layers. The agents' header and panels show while the agents layer is on. The agents data comes from the public policies on this site (/abp/), with its "as of" time.
- **Two looks.** One button switches the Universe between light points (the default, best for the whole map) and the glass balloons in the team's T-shirt colours (best with a few layers on).
- **Kiosk view for the stand** (security review finding F1). A stand screen shows only Demo, Policy, Evidence, Your company and Report. It has no Control, Plan, leads, emails, other visitors or menu. It is locked and unlocked with a 4-digit PIN, and after 3 minutes untouched it clears the visitor. The PIN is a screen lock, not a security boundary: the stand computers still need their own accounts with no connectors (F2).
- **Deleting and retention** (F5).
  - The operator can delete a visitor, in two steps. A visitor can delete their own visit from their report, also on a stand screen.
  - Visitors without consent are wiped at the end of the day. Contacts with consent are kept for the follow-up, at most 90 days, then wiped.
  - The brain forgets the deleted visit too.
  - The deletion log keeps no names, companies, emails or ids.
  - Visitors from before the rule are offered for wiping, never wiped silently.
