# Hot leads go to Nimay or Dinis: the rule (draft, WS-29)

**Draft, not yet posted to the team.** Vicky will post it in the team's WhatsApp group before the summit starts, for Nimay and Dinis to confirm. Written on 4 November 2026 from what Nimay said at his meeting with Vicky that day (16:30 UTC), with Vicky's additions. The issue's aim is that "when a lead is hot, Nimay or Dinis meets them (or is part of it), within a day" (board/WS-29).

## What counts as hot

A lead is hot if they:

- ask for a **demo**, **pricing** or a **meeting**;
- are a **decision-maker**; or
- describe a **problem RiskMandate solves today**.

## The rule

When someone you talk to is a hot lead:

1. **Capture four things:**
   - their **role and company**;
   - their **interest level**: high or medium;
   - the **area** they are interested in;
   - **where they are with agent or AI security**.
2. **Post it in the team's WhatsApp group**, straight away, with the post below.
3. **Nimay or Dinis decides within a day:** Zoom or in person.
4. **A booked meeting goes into the private WS-23 vault**, with its fields.

**A lead's name and details never go on the site or into this repository.** The WhatsApp group is internal to the team, and the private vault is where a booked meeting is kept.

## The post, to copy into the WhatsApp group

> Hot lead: [name], [role] at [company]. Interest: [high / medium]. Area: [what they want]. Agent or AI security: [where they are]. How to reach: [how]. Met by [who], [where].
