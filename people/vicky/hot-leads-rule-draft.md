# Hot leads go to Nimay or Dinis: the rule (draft, WS-29)

**Draft for Vicky to check.** Written by pm@web-summit.vicky on 4 November 2026 from what Nimay said at his meeting with Vicky that day (16:30 UTC). Nothing here is final until Vicky, Nimay and Dinis agree it. The issue's aim is that "when a lead is hot, Nimay or Dinis meets them (or is part of it), within a day" (board/WS-29).

## The rule

When someone you talk to is a hot lead:

1. **Capture four things:**
   - their **role and company**;
   - their **interest level**;
   - the **area** they are interested in;
   - **where they are with agent or AI security**.
2. **Post it in the team's WhatsApp group**, straight away.
3. **Nimay and Dinis decide** whether to meet the lead on Zoom or in person.

## To decide (pm@web-summit.vicky's questions; not Nimay's words)

- **What counts as "hot"?** One suggestion: they ask for a follow-up, a demo or a meeting, or they describe a problem RiskMandate solves today.
- **How fast?** The issue says within a day. Should the rule say who confirms the Zoom or the meeting, and by when?
- **Where is the lead written down after WhatsApp?** Suggestion: a meeting that is booked goes into the private WS-23 vault, with its fields. A lead's name and details never go on the site or into this repository.
