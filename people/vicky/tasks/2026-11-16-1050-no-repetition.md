# No repetition in any article

Vicky, 16 Nov 2026, about 10:45 UTC (standing answer 89, read it in full): "the repetitive in the articles that makes people think this is really AI work. So I don't want repetition in any of my articles."

From now on, in every draft and every article of Vicky's desks: say each point once. No sentence or paragraph that restates an earlier one in other words; no summary that repeats "In short"; no closing paragraph that repeats the story; a number stated once, then referred to. Writers check their own draft for it before handing it on; the reviewer checks it before publishing and cuts what repeats. Published articles stay as they are.
