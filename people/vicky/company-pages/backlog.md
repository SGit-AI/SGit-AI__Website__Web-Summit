**Done** means, in the existing Replit structure and no new sections: `desks/journalist-vicky/companies/<id>.md` with "## About" (standing answer 23) and "## At Web Summit 2026" (each participation with its Web Summit source; "Media only" only where a Web Summit page says so); reviewed `status: ready`, released, and checked live at 390 px and desktop. "From the newsroom" appears by itself where one of our published articles links to the page; nobody edits a published article to add a link.

**How a batch works:** the journalist takes only the rows of its batch, one file per company, pushes them `status: in-review` together and sets their status here to `in review`; the reviewer checks the batch, releases the Ready files in one push, checks each page live, and sets `done` (or `fix`, or `skipped` with why). A company whose sources are not enough is `skipped` with the reason, never filled.
