# Plan: the reporter role

At Vicky's word (11 Nov 2026, about 19:00 UTC: "Plan the reporter role, reports to vault A only"). Not started. Starts after Friday, from programme's session (see the repurposing hand-overs).

**What it does:** finds evidence of what agents actually do, at other organisations (standing answer 29): primary sources that show a real task, workflow, human decision, failure or result; builds a roadmap per candidate (what is established, by which source, what is missing, who could confirm it publicly).

**What it never does:** write articles (the journalists do), publish, read the CRM, the shortlist or vault B, or make machine reads of the sites in RULES-11NOV.

**Where its work goes:** internal reports only to vault A (`reports/reporter/<date>-<topic>.md`), audited before each push. The curator reads them with vault A's read key and commissions from them, as it does from the research vault. On the team site: one public line per run in `people/vicky/reports/reporter.md`, counts only.

**Its place in the flow:** researcher (public captures) and reporter (evidence roadmaps) both feed the curator; the curator commissions; journalists write; the reviewer checks and publishes under R-PUB-1.

**Schedule (proposed):** twice a day, 30 minutes before the curator's runs. Model: Sonnet.
