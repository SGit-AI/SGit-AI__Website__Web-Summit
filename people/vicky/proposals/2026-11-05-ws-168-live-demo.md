# Live demo at the stand: from a conversation to a page in minutes

*Proposal for WS-168, by Vicky, 5 November 2026. Draft for discussion.*

## Why
The best demo of RiskMandate is our own agents at work. An article takes hours and needs the newsroom's checks. A page made for the person in front of us takes a few minutes, and they see the agent working for them. Articles can still follow later, as an option.

## How it works at the summit
1. **Show our own team.** Our command centres, the newsroom our agents build, and the story page: real agents with their own policies, at work this week.
2. **Ask: try it for you?** "Want to see what an agent makes for your case?" Record nothing without a yes.
3. **They talk, the agent listens.** The person describes their situation by voice, in the Claude app on our phone (a "Web Summit demo" project).
4. **The agent makes something for them.** It starts by showing its own policy: what it can do, what it may do, and what it won't. Then an infographic or a short page about their own agents: what they can reach, what they're allowed to do, and the gap between the two. Private link, shared only with that person.
5. **Pricing or a meeting → hot lead (WS-29).** The agent says Nimay or Dinis will be in touch. Once WS-21 has price ranges, the agent can give those too. Nothing is sold on site.
6. **After the conversation.** A voice memo to our own agent for the follow-up (WS-61). An article for the newsroom only if they agree, through the usual newsroom route.

## How long it takes
About 5 to 8 minutes in all, to be confirmed in a test:
- Show our team: 1 to 2 minutes
- Ask: half a minute
- They talk: 1 to 2 minutes
- The agent's policy, then the page or infographic: 1 to 3 minutes
- Look at it together, pricing or a meeting: 1 minute

To keep it fast: one fixed template in the instructions (three boxes: reach, mandate, gap), so the agent only fills it in. Keep talking while it works. Have one finished example ready in case the network is slow.

## What each person needs
- The Claude app on their phone, with a "Web Summit demo" project. A project chat is enough: no Claude Code agent, no keys, no schedule.
- One shared instruction text for that project: who we are, the demo agent's own policy (reach, mandate, what stops it), the template, and the rules below. The offer from WS-21 is added when it's ready.
- Links ready on the phone: our command centres, the newsroom, the story page.

## Rules
- Ask before recording anyone's voice.
- No contact details in what the agent makes.
- Nothing about the person goes on our sites or in the repositories.
- Say only what riskmandate.ai says. Price ranges only from WS-21.
