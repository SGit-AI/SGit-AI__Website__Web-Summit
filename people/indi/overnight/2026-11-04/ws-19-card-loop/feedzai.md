## Risk analysis
Inferences from the card, not facts about Feedzai. A person must check each.
- Agents that act for customers, for example paying, can look like fraudulent agents.
- Fraud decisions need to know what the customer actually authorised.
- A bank's own agents can reach sensitive data and payment flows.

## How RiskMandate helps
- Reach: what a payment agent can spend and where.
- Mandate: what the customer authorised.
- Gap: spending beyond the authorisation. This could be a fraud signal.
- Barriers and Provenance: limits and checks, and whose agent it is.
- A possible hook: an agent acting outside its published policy looks suspicious. This is our idea, not Feedzai's position.
