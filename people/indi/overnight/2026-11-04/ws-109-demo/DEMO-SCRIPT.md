# WS-109: the two-minute demo, "the grant, not the prompt" (DRAFT for Indi to check)

By messaging@web-summit.indi, 4 November 2026. Agent draft, not yet reviewed by a person. Not tested. The demo idea comes from `board/WS-109-*.md` and the newsroom article "The grant, not the prompt". I could not read the article in this session, so check every step against it.

## What it shows

Ask a chat that has nothing to do with email: "What emails do I have?" It can answer, because the access was set at the click that granted it, not by anything in the prompt. The point: **the access is the risk, and it was decided earlier by a person.** That is RiskMandate's Reach.

## Two-minute script

| Time | Screen | Words | The one line to remember |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:15 | A chat window, empty. | "This is an ordinary AI chat. I will ask it something that has nothing to do with email." | "Ordinary chat." |
| 0:15 to 0:35 | Type: "What emails do I have?" | "Watch. I did not mention a mailbox in the prompt." | "No mailbox in the prompt." |
| 0:35 to 0:55 | The chat lists the emails. | "It answered. Nobody told it how. At some point a person clicked Allow." | "A person clicked Allow." |
| 0:55 to 1:20 | Open the connection settings, where the grant was made. | "That click is the whole policy. Not the words I type. The access was set here, once." | "The grant, not the prompt." |
| 1:20 to 1:45 | The RiskMandate record (the Reach, Mandate, Gap table). | "RiskMandate writes this down. Reach: what it can touch. Mandate: what it may do. Gap: the difference." | "Reach. Mandate. Gap." |
| 1:45 to 2:00 | The stand line and the QR. | "If you run, buy or sell agents, ask: do you know the reach of yours?" | "Know their reach." |

The "Reach, Mandate, Gap" record on screen: use the 53-second video `content/videos.md` (Reach, Mandate, Gap) or the Record card's REACH / AUTHORISED / GAP table (`people/indi/synthetic/cards-run-2/findings.md`, "What held").

## What can go wrong, and the fallback

| Risk | Fallback |
|---|---|
| No signal | Play the downloaded video and say the script from memory. |
| The chat says it cannot see email | Show the settings screen first: "This is the click that is missing." The lesson holds the other way round. |
| The chat shows real private mail | Stop the demo. Use only a test mailbox (checklist item one). |
| A visitor asks if it is shipped | Say what is true: the demo shows how access is granted today. SHarness and signed email are not shipped, never present them as such. |
| A visitor asks "so how do I fix it?" | "That is the policy: write down reach and mandate, then check the barriers." Hand over the card. |
| Over time | End at 1:20 with "The grant, not the prompt." |
