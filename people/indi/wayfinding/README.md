# Wayfinding: from where I am to my registration point

Dinis, arriving at his hotel on 9 November: *"How can we have the technology and the workflows and the multiple environments that I'm in so that I can be given a page that shows my hotel, the registration, and the map, and at least some kind of line, even if the line is not perfect."* He asked us to describe the problem first, then several solutions, from the simplest to the most advanced, across the platforms we have. Then pick the simplest one that is useful, ship it, and iterate.

**The short answer:** the MVP is one public page with the two registration points, their hours today, and an "open in Maps" button for each. A "from where I am" button draws a straight line on a simple map, on the phone only. The Team app shows websites, so the same page appears in the app with no app release.
