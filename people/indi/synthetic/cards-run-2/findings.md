# Findings, run 2 (the six round-2 designs)

Grouped by cost. Invented personas; "measured" means checked by a tool.

## Blocking

1. **Four of the six designs never say what RiskMandate does.** Pass, Boardroom, Seal and Signature: "no" from 4 or 5 of 5 personas each. Only Record and Terminal state a category ("AGENT BEHAVIOUR POLICIES"). An investor will not Google (Hannah).
2. **Boardroom's line names no subject.** "Your customer will ask. Have an answer." never says what the question is, and the front never mentions agents; two personas read it as generic consultancy marketing.
3. **"Ask me what my agents did today."** (Dinis's line on Seal and Signature) read to the bank CISO as a cute question with no product in it.

## Costly

4. **The role lines are internal roles, not titles.** "The message, outreach, and the factory of examples" (Indi), "Ideas, outreach, local, and video" (Silvio), "Operator" (Dinis): none is a title a journalist can print or a buyer can place. All five asked for a plain job title.
5. **"Find us at our stand on Thursday 12 November" has no hall or stand number**, and it is the last day of a four-day summit; two asked why only that day.
6. **Seal, Record and Signature never say "Web Summit" or "Lisbon"**, so a card found a week later has no context.
7. **Record says what the gap is, not what RiskMandate does about it** (measure, document or enforce?). Asked by Aiko and Marta; Marta wants the frameworks named (DORA, the EU AI Act).
8. **No contact but the QR**, on every design, and the page the QR opens still has run 1's three blockers (measured on all seven pages, 4 November about 16:00 UTC; WS-146).
9. **"Talk to my agent"** made the CISO suspect a bot (Pass, Terminal).

## What held

- **Record's REACH / AUTHORISED / GAP table** is the clearest reason to believe in the whole set: "keep it in whatever design wins" (Hannah); "the only card that tells me what the product checks" (Marta).
- **Terminal's "Building is not authorising."** was the best single line for the engineer, and second-best for three others.
- **The printed URL beside the QR** lets a careful reader check where the code goes. *Measured:* 42 of 42 QR codes decode to the right page.

## The one change each would make to their pick

- Marta (Record): name the audit, e.g. "mapped to DORA and the EU AI Act", and print the company name in words on the front.
- Kwame (Terminal): one line on what he can plug in (a policy layer, an API), and the stand number next to the date.
- Hannah (Record): one company line naming the buyer and the stage, plus "Web Summit Lisbon" and the stand number.
- Rafael (Record): a printable title with the company, a press contact and the stand number.
- Aiko (Record): what RiskMandate does about the gap, "Web Summit Lisbon" with the stand number, and a plain job title.

## Suggested (for the designer and the team's vote)

- Vote for **Record**, or Record for risk, policy and executives with **Terminal** for engineers.
- On the winner: a plain job title per person, "Web Summit Lisbon" and the stand number, and one line on what RiskMandate does about the gap.
- Before printing: WS-146 (the page every card leads to).
