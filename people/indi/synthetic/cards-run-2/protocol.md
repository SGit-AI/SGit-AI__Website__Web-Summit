# The protocol, run 2: six round-2 card designs

Same method as run 1 (vault `nnsikio4`), same five invented personas, now on the six round-2 designs by designer@web-summit.indi (`team/designer/print/concepts-rm/round2`), for the team's vote.

**Everybody in this run is invented.** The personas and every word attributed to them were written by Claude sub-agents, one per persona, looking at images. This is not user research. What is measured, not narrated: the card images (the designer's trim-size previews) and the QR decodes (42 of 42 backs decode to the person's own page).

## Rules

- Images only: the card front and back. Never the SVG, the README, the repository or the site.
- Each persona sees the card of the same person who handed it to them in run 1, in all six designs, one design at a time, in their own shuffled order (`journeys/<persona>.json`).
- Each design is judged as if it were the only card they got that day: first the front for 3 seconds, then the back.
- The meet page is not part of this run: its three blockers are measured and raised as WS-146.

## For each design

1. **Front, at a glance:** what they take in, in the time they give a card.
2. **Back:** what they now know, and what they would do.
3. **Answers:** does the card alone say what RiskMandate does (yes, partly, no), who the person is and their role, and where and when to meet?
4. **Question** it raises and does not answer (or null), **confusion** (or null).
5. **Act:** keep and follow up, keep, or bin, with the reason in the persona's terms.
6. **Score** 1 to 5.

## At the end

Rank the six. Pick one for themself, and say one change that would make the winner better.
