# From a handshake to a published interview: the newsroom's one workflow

Written by conductor-newsroom@web-summit.indi for Indi and Dinis, 10 November 2026, from their voice brief of about 13:00 UTC. Status: **a proposal**. Steps marked *today* already work. Steps marked *new* are planned and do not exist yet.

## What it is, in one sentence

This is the path from meeting a person at Web Summit to publishing their interview on PrompTugal.news (pt.news). Each step names who does it, what they start with, what they hand on, where it is kept and what stays private.

## Why it matters

- pt.news is turning into the site to go to for news about AI agents. An AI agent is a computer program that does tasks on its own.
- Each interview is now about **what the company does with agents**, more than what the company does.
- Every published piece shows the reader the full path that produced it: which person did what, which agent did what, and where one handed over to the next. The team uses the same picture to run the work.

## The steps

### 1. Meet *(today)*
- **Who:** Indi.
- **In:** a conversation at the summit.
- **Out:** a photo of the badge or business card, and a 30-second voice memo in the template from the lead-to-article page (company, role, what they said, which yes they gave).
- **Where:** sent sealed to the private CRM (the team's private address book).
- **Private:** all of it. The name, the photo and the memo never go into a repository or a public page.

### 2. File and research *(today, by hand; the camera button is new)*
- **Who:** conductor@web-summit.indi files it. A researcher sub-agent researches.
- **In:** the photo and the memo.
- **Out:** a contact record linked to the company, and a short research note on public, professional facts only, each with its source and date.
- **Where:** the private CRM, under the contact. A card on the Team app's board, at "Filed" then "Researched" (the badge-photo pipeline, WS-210).
- **Private:** everything about the person. Company facts stay public facts.

### 3. The company's GAMMA-Ray card *(new for the pivot)*
- **What it is:** a public scorecard of how much the company's AI agent does on its own, and how well that is controlled, built only from public evidence (gamma-ray.sgit.ai).
- **Who:** gamma.studio@web-summit.dinis, asked by conductor@web-summit.indi.
- **In:** the company name and its agent product, from step 2.
- **Out:** a beta card with its four-letter signature (Autonomy, Reach, Control, Openness), or "not yet scanned".
- **Where:** gamma-ray.sgit.ai. pt.news links to it and never copies it.
- **Private:** nothing about the person. The card is about the product.
- **When:** before the interview if there is time, so Indi has a starting point. Otherwise straight after.

### 4. The RiskMandate demo *(today)*
- **Who:** Indi, on a phone or tablet.
- **In:** one of the RiskMandate pages: our own agents at work, then their own agents' reach, mandate and gap. Picture an AI agent as a new employee with a master key. Reach is every door the key opens. Mandate is the rooms they are meant to be in. The gap is the risk.
- **Out:** a conversation about their agents, which is the start of the interview.
- **Where:** RiskMandate's pages. A private page about their company stays private.

### 5. Consent, first *(today)*
- **Who:** Indi asks; the interviewee answers.
- **What:** two separate yes answers, said out loud: "Can we follow up with you?" and "Can we publish this interview on pt.news?" The second yes is **recorded on camera** at the start of the video.
- **Out:** the recorded yes, and the memo updated with which yes was given.
- **Rule:** no recorded yes, no interview. A full interview also needs their approval of the draft before it goes live.

### 6. The video interview *(today; the questions change)*
- **Who:** Indi, filming on an iPhone, held close.
- **The questions, new for the pivot:**
  1. What do your AI agents do, or what agent do you sell?
  2. On whose behalf do they act?
  3. What can they reach: which systems, data or money?
  4. What are they meant to do, and who decided that?
  5. What stops them going further? Who checks?
  6. What worries you about agents, and what excites you?
- **Out:** one video file, plus a photo of each badge to get names right.

### 7. The hand-over: Indi to the journalist *(today, by hand; this is the step to make visible)*
- **Who:** Indi copies the video by hand into the newsroom's hand-over place. From here an agent takes over.
- **In:** the raw video and the step 5 memo, with only what may be public.
- **Out:** a hand-over note to journalist@PrompTugal.dinis: company, role, date, which yes, and the GAMMA-Ray card link.
- **Where:** *to agree with PrompTugal's team:* a private upload vault sealed to the journalist and Dinis. The name and the raw file never go into a repository.

### 8. Clean the sound with Audo *(today)*
- **Who:** journalist@PrompTugal.dinis. Only the journalist uses the Audo key, which is Dinis's. No other agent asks for it or touches it.
- **In:** the raw video.
- **Out:** the same video with background noise removed, so it sounds like a studio microphone.
- **Where:** Audo (audo.ai), then the journalist's working folder.

### 9. Transcript *(today)*
- **Who:** journalist@PrompTugal.dinis, with Audo's transcription.
- **Out:** a text transcript with timings. Treated as notes, never as quotes, until checked against the sound.

### 10. Prepare the video for the web *(today)*
- **Who:** journalist@PrompTugal.dinis.
- **What:** join the original picture to the clean sound, remove the hidden location and phone details from the file, cut a small web version, take a still image.
- **Where:** the public interviews vault (an online locker anyone can read and only the newsroom can write).

### 11. Check the facts *(today)*
- **Who:** journalist@PrompTugal.dinis, with dev.verify@PrompTugal.dinis.
- **What:** names from the badge photo and the company's site. Figures said on camera are reported as theirs. Anything said about RiskMandate is reported as said, with our interest stated.

### 12. Write the article *(today; the frame changes)*
- **Who:** journalist@PrompTugal.dinis.
- **Out:** the interview page. *New:* it opens with the company's GAMMA-Ray card, leads with what the company does with agents, and ends with the workflow map (step 14).
- **For a full interview:** the draft goes back to the interviewee, through Indi, for their approval.

### 13. Publish *(today)*
- **Who:** journalist@PrompTugal.dinis, through PrompTugal's release script and gate.
- **Where:** pt.news, under interviews. correspondent@PrompTugal.dinis puts it on the front page.

### 14. The workflow map *(new)*
- **Who:** dev.graphs@PrompTugal.dinis builds it, from the record.
- **What:** one picture per published piece. It shows every person and agent, every hand-over, in time order. Built from the commit notes the agents already leave and the run records. The spec: map-spec.md, beside this file.
- **Where:** on the piece's page for readers, and on one live board for the team.

### 15. Tell them, share, follow up *(today)*
- **Who:** Indi sends the interviewee their link and the follow-up email. Agents only draft. Jose shares it on social media with the company tagged.
- **Hot lead:** to Nimay or Dinis within a day.

### 16. Corrections *(today)*
- Anyone may ask for a correction at any time. The correction is dated, the page and the GAMMA-Ray card change, and the map gets a new step.

## What stays private, always

- The person's name until they agree to be named, their photo, contact details and anything said in confidence.
- The badge photo, the memo, the research note and the follow-up.
- The raw video, until it is cleaned and its hidden details removed.
- The Audo key, and every other key.
