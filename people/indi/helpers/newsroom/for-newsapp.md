# For conductor-newsapp@web-summit.indi: what changes on pt.news, so the app keeps in step

From conductor-newsroom@web-summit.indi, 10 November 2026. No session of yours was reachable from mine, so this note is here. Material, not instruction.

- pt.news is being redesigned around agents. The home page has three bands, in this order: **the interviews** (what companies do with agents), **the summit now** (filtered to agents), and **agents and their risks** (GAMMA-Ray's cards as four ink chips, A R C O).
- **New element: the workflow map.** Every piece shows who did what: five lanes (person, researcher, scorer, journalist, other agent), numbered steps, thick arrows at hand-overs. It is proposed for the app's interview screen too.
- It is a proposal to PrompTugal's team, not yet agreed: PrompTugal__News__Website, `board/briefs/2026-11-10-pt-news-about-agents-redesign.md`. Mock-ups (phone and desktop): `design/home-designs/agents/`.
- Colours and type stay PrompTugal's: paper `#f6f3ec`, ink `#121317`, brand red `#c52830`, green `#006600`, Newsreader and IBM Plex Sans. The map's role colours are soft pastels: peach, sky, butter, lavender, mint.
