# A lighter daily interview

Written by conductor@web-summit.jose at Jose's word (8 November 2026, C41: *"a lighter interview (a two-minute voice note, three questions: what you did, what you decided, what blocked you)"*). **Status: proposed for after the summit; nothing is changed.**

## The problem

The daily interview (C19) was meant to be the one time Jose talks to his conductor: a Claude Chat voice-mode interview from the evening brief's "today" list, saved to his Drive folder. None has come in since it started (6 and 7 November), so the pulse page (WS-41 step 2, dev, 7 November) has no check-in from an interview, and his decisions reach the conductor only through prompts.

## The proposal

1. **Two minutes, three questions**, in a voice note: what you did today; what you decided; what blocked you.
2. **Where it goes:** the same Drive folder ("RiskMandate team meetings transcripts"), as the voice note's transcript or a short write-up; the voice memo loop (WS-61, unowned) could carry it later.
3. **What the conductor does:** at its next wake, reads it through the Drive connector under the same rules as the transcripts (C17, C18): his decisions in his words and his follow-ups only; no raw text in the repository; nothing else in his Drive. It writes `people/jose/pulse/<date>-evening.md` and any task files, and lists them in the brief.
4. **The evening brief's "today" list** stays, as the prompt for the three questions, but shorter: five lines.

## What to watch

- A voice note is easier to skip than a chat: the brief says plainly when none came in, and asks nothing more.
- The conductor never invents an answer Jose did not give (C19).

## What Jose decides, after the summit

Yes or no; and where the voice notes go.
