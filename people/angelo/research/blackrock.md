**Who they are and what they do**
- A global investment manager; its own words: "As a global investment manager and fiduciary to our clients, our purpose at BlackRock is to help everyone experience financial well-being." Source: [blackrock.com/corporate](https://www.blackrock.com/corporate), read 2026-11-08.
- The site's top navigation names three brands: BlackRock, iShares and Aladdin. It also says "Since 1999, we've been a leading provider of financial technology", which is the Aladdin side. Same source.
- Its pages carry regulated entities in the UK (BlackRock Investment Management (UK) Limited, FCA-authorised) and the Netherlands (BlackRock (Netherlands) B.V., AFM-authorised). Same source.

**Recent public news** (homepage items, read 2026-11-08; the page shows no dates for them)
- 2026 Midyear Global Outlook, "Abundance vs. scarcity": asks whether AI lifts growth or whether scarcity of power, labour, capital and materials dominates.
- A press release: a national US$100 million philanthropic initiative, BlackRock Future Builders, to train and support 50,000 American workers in skilled trades, funded by the BlackRock Foundation.
- Larry Fink's 2026 Chairman's Letter to Investors, "Growing with your country".
