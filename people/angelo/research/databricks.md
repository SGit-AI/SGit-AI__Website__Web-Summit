**Who they are and what they do**
- A "unified data and AI platform that runs both analytical and operational workloads on one open, governed foundation". Source: [databricks.com](https://www.databricks.com), read 2026-11-08.
- Products named on the homepage: Lakebase (serverless Postgres in the lakehouse, "for applications that scale"), Agent Bricks (to "build high-quality, production-ready AI agents grounded in your data"), Genie (conversational analytics, "your data-aware AI partner"), Unity Catalog (one governance layer across "data, models, dashboards and agents"), Unity Gateway ("govern enterprise AI with unified access, cost control, observability and guardrails"), Lakeflow (pipelines) and a data warehouse. Same source.
- Its own figures: over 60% of the Fortune 500 and over 20,000 customers; a Leader in Gartner's 2026 Magic Quadrant for AI Platforms for DSML. Same source (company claims, not checked).
- The navigation lists AI Agents, AI Governance and Cybersecurity as solution areas. Same source.

**Recent public news** (homepage, read 2026-11-08; the items show no year)
- Events: "Data + AI Live" on 11 November; a virtual event on agentic apps on 10 to 12 November; a Data + AI Summit page (15 to 18 June, San Francisco) that says "Last chance to register".
- An executive panel, "How CISOs Are Rethinking SecOps", and a security product described as "open agentic SIEM built for the AI era".

**Why it matters for RiskMandate and PrompTugal.news** (my reading)
- It sells both the agents and the governance layer for them (Agent Bricks, Unity Gateway's guardrails). That makes it a natural place to ask how an agent's mandate and its limits are expressed and enforced, and a candidate subject for a piece on agent access to enterprise data.
