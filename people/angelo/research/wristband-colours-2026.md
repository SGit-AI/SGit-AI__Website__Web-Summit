**Read this first: I found no public source that says what any 2026 wristband or lanyard colour means.** Web Summit's own public pages (websummit.com, its tickets, ticket-guide, attendee-experience and blog pages; the speaker portal; the partner and media hubs that could be read) do not mention colours. Several of those pages are rendered by script and gave little readable text, so a legend may exist behind a login (the app, the attendee portal) that I cannot read. A piece for readers should therefore be built on what Angelo sees on site, not on this file alone.

## Confirmed (each from a public source)

| Fact | Source | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Attendees receive a wristband and a lanyard at registration, and are expected to wear both each day (also recommended for Night Summit). Tickets are personal: only the person on the ticket can collect them. | Web Summit's partner hub and registration guides, as returned by search ([venue access and information](https://sites.google.com/websummit.com/lisbonpartnerhub2025/event-overview/venue-access-and-information), [registration essentials](https://lisbon.cilabs.com/essentials/registration-essentials)) | 9 Nov 2026 |
| Each lanyard carries a QR code; scanning it offers to connect with that attendee. The app's lead scanner adds accepted scans to a lead list. | same guides, via search result text | 9 Nov 2026 |
| Impact startups are marked with an "impact badge" on their exhibition board and in the app (not a lanyard colour). | Web Summit partner hub text, via search result | 9 Nov 2026 |
| A lost lanyard or wristband costs 100 euros. | PrompTugal.news's "your first day" article, cited in [registration-2026.md](registration-2026.md) | 8 Nov 2026 |

## A colour mapping, from 2014 only: not confirmed for any later year

One public account, from the Dublin summit of November 2014, a first-hand comparison with SXSW by an attendee ([LinkedIn Pulse, 14 Nov 2014](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/20141114095645-135497070-web-summit-vs-sxsw)), says each attendee segment had its own badge colour:

| Segment (2014) | Badge colour (2014) |
|---|---|
| Media | dark green |
| Investors | red |
| ALPHA startups | pink |
| BETA startups | bluish-green |
| "Other" general attendee | yellow |

The same author says the colours made investors and media easy to spot and, in his words, "a target" for startups, and that some events admitted only one segment. Twelve years and several cities later, **I cannot say any of this still holds.** Do not print these colours as this year's.

## Not confirmed (nothing public found)

- Any 2026 colour for: general attendee, startup (ALPHA, BETA, GROWTH), investor, media, speaker, partner, staff, volunteer, VIP.
- Whether the wristband colour differs by day or by access (Night Summit, side events) rather than by person type.
- Whether lanyard and wristband colours mean the same thing.
- Generic advice that "colours are set by each organiser" is true of events in general ([wristbandexpress guide](https://www.wristbandexpress.com/blogs/news/wristband-color-meanings-guide)) and is not a Web Summit source. Lists such as AWS re:Invent's lanyard colours are other events and do not apply.
- Searches (English; one extended pass) for reports from the last days, in news and public posts, found none that describe 2026 colours.
