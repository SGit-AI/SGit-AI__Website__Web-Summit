**Who they are and what they do**
- Makes a Training Management System (TMS) for instructor-led and virtual instructor-led training: scheduling, instructor and resource management, conflict alerts, automated communications, revenue tracking and reporting. It positions itself beside an LMS, not as a replacement. Source: [getadministrate.com](https://www.getadministrate.com), read 2026-11-08.
- For enterprise training teams and training providers running many live classes; the site names Roche Diagnostics, Siemens Healthineers, TÜV SÜD, Maersk Training and Royal Caribbean Group as customers. Same source, read 2026-11-08.
- Capterra lists it with integrations to 20+ tools (Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Gmail) and a 4.3 rating from 41 reviews. Source: [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/136896/Administrate/), via a search summary, read 2026-11-08.
