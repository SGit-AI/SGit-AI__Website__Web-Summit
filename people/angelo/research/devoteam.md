**Who they are and what they do**
- A French technology consulting company working with private and public clients, independent of any single vendor; headquartered in Levallois-Perret, founded 1995. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devoteam_Group), read 2026-11-07.
- Partners named there: AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, ServiceNow, Snowflake, Databricks. About 11,000 employees and about €1.13 billion revenue in 2023 (the article's own figures differ slightly). Same source.

**Recent public news**
- Strategy: the "Amplify" plan, launched in 2024, aims at AI-driven consulting, €2 billion revenue by 2028 and half of revenue from AI projects. Source: search summary of Devoteam coverage (e.g. [Computable](https://www.computable.nl/?p=370761)), read 2026-11-07; I did not read the full article.
- 27 January 2026: Deiser listed as its latest acquisition. Source: [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/devoteam/financials), via search summary, read 2026-11-07.
- A report that Solutions by stc bought 40 percent of Devoteam Middle East; date not shown. Source: [GCC Business News](https://www.gccbusinessnews.com/tag/devoteam-middle-east/), read 2026-11-07.
- Earlier deals: Singularity Digital Enterprise, a Lisbon data and AI firm (October 2023, [Essential Business](https://www.essential-business.pt/?p=24106)); Inlogiq, Spanish automation (December 2024). Wikipedia lists the rest.

**Why it matters for RiskMandate and PrompTugal.news**
