## Tuesday 10 November

1. **12:30-13:15, Masterclass 3: "Trust issues: Letting AI agents into production safely" (hosted by Delinea).** Agents in production under identity and privilege control: the core of his work. (ws26-s06)
2. **10:50-11:10, Stage 7: "Your AI agent just got a passport"** (Benjamin Faes, RWS). Agent identity and credentials: matches the team's keys and ABPs. (ws26-s04)
3. **11:50-12:10, Centre Stage: "The agentic internet: Who controls the web?"** (Matthew Prince, Cloudflare; Nick Thompson, The Atlantic). What an agent may reach. (ws26-s01)
4. **14:25-14:55, Stage 4: "Who takes the fall for autonomous AI?"** then **14:45-14:55 "Double agents"** (Jordi Romero, Factorial) in the same room: liability and the insider agent. (ws26-s08, s09)
5. **16:00-16:20, Stage 13: "What does a self-driving company look like?"** (Michele Catasta, Replit): a company run by agents, close to his hundred-agent story. (ws26-s51)

Clashes: 10:50 also has Chat Control 2.0 (Stage 11); 12:30 also has "Regulating big tech" (Centre Stage, Irish DPC) and the Log360 masterclass; 14:00 Developer Lounge session on agent context if you want a developer one. Walk time between 12:10 and 12:30 is the only tight gap.

**Evening:** 17:00-19:00 Superconnectors Lisbon @ Web Summit (venue not given on the page: se21), then 19:00-22:00 An Evening with Elastic (Palacete dos Condes de Monte Real, se14; Elastic runs an AI-security masterclass that morning). The Aubay "Building a Tech Hub" happy hour (17:30, IBM Portugal, se10) is the Portuguese-tech alternative.
