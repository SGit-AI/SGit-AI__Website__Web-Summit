**Who they are and what they do**
- "The all-in-one AI restaurant management system", with modules for business intelligence, inventory, workforce, payroll and a capital (financing) offer. Source: [nory.ai](https://nory.ai), read 2026-11-08.
- A "crew of AI assistants running your operation": forecasting (revenue and covers by the hour), scheduling, customer reviews, ordering ("creates and sends supplier purchase orders"), compliance, payroll (builds pay runs from real shifts) and invoicing (matches invoices to purchase orders and flags discrepancies "before you pay"). Same source.
- Sold to independent brands, franchise networks, multi-location brands and enterprise groups. Same source.
- Its own claims, unchecked: food waste cut by 50%, labour costs by 20%, 100+ admin hours a month saved (page description); Rocksalt's labour costs down 7%; sales forecast accuracy 97.5%; Black Sheep Coffee scaling across 130 sites (success stories). Same source.

**Recent public news:** none read; the homepage shows success stories without dates.

**Why it matters for RiskMandate and PrompTugal.news** (my reading)
- These assistants act on money and obligations: they order from suppliers, run payroll and check invoices before payment. That is agent authority over spending in many small businesses, which is what a mandate is for, and a ready, concrete story for readers.

**Gaps:** dates, funding and customer numbers beyond its own claims, how its assistants' actions are approved or limited, summit presence.
