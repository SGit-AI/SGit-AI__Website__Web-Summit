## The plain answer

- **Airport desk: likely smooth.** Two separate guides, for 2022 and for 2025, say the airport registration usually has no queue or a manageable one. Sources: [freshestweb, 13 Nov 2022](https://freshestweb.com/en/blog/web-summit-tips-tricks-guide/) ("the queues and waiting times are manageable"); [FusionAuth, 2025](https://fusionauth.io/blog/fusionauth-web-summit) ("usually has no queues"). Both are one author's experience, not counts.
- **Venue (Pavilhão de Portugal and the gates), first morning: likely long.** The only waits with a number are for the venue: "1 hour and more" on the opening day in 2022, and a queue that "backs up into the plaza" in the first days. The 2026 guides all name Tuesday 10 November, 08:30 to 10:00, as the crush.
- **Best for Angelo:** collect at the airport desk on arrival if the flight lands between 07:30 and 22:00 on 8 or 9 November (it is open 07:30 to 22:00 both days, 07:30 to 15:00 on 10 November). If he is already in town, go to Pavilhão de Portugal on **Monday 9 November in the early afternoon**, before the evening's opening night, not on Tuesday morning. Either way, do not collect on Tuesday morning and then queue to enter.
- **How sure:** medium for the airport (two independent accounts, different years, but anecdotal); low to medium for the venue (old numbers, 2026 headcount comparable, no live 2026 report). The organiser publishes no wait times.
- **Ask:** when does Angelo land? That decides airport or Pavilhão.

## 1. This year's points and hours

Start: PrompTugal.news, ["Web Summit Lisbon: your first day, from airport to arena"](https://promptugal.news/articles/your-first-day-at-web-summit.html), published 6 November 2026, read 2026-11-08. It says "times may change, per Web Summit". It cites Web Summit's registration page, which I fetched on 2026-11-08: the page has the title and dates (9 to 12 November) but no hours in its text, so I cannot confirm the hours at Web Summit's own page; the article's hours are the only complete set I found.

| Place | Hours (per the article) |
|---|---|
| Lisbon Airport, Terminal 1 | 7 Nov 09:30 to 22:00; 8 and 9 Nov 07:30 to 22:00; 10 Nov 07:30 to 15:00 |
| Pavilhão de Portugal (next to the venue) | 7 and 8 Nov 12:00 to 18:00; 9 Nov 12:00 to 22:00; 10 and 11 Nov 07:30 to 17:00; 12 Nov 07:30 to 16:45 |

- Bring photo ID and the ticket (QR under "My ticket" in the app). A lost lanyard or wristband costs 100 euros (same article).
- Getting there: Oriente metro station is about 300 m from the MEO Arena; the red line runs from the airport to Oriente. Metro de Lisboa plans extra red-line trains if its fleet allows, for about 75,000 participants ([Executive Digest, 4 Nov 2026](https://executivedigest.sapo.pt/web-summit-2026-metro-de-lisboa-prepara-reforco-de-comboios-e-vigilancia-para-receber-75-mil-participantes/), read 2026-11-08, via the article above).
- A third-party guide says the organiser had not published reliable 2026 locations or hours when it was last updated on 5 October ([The Lisbon Concierge](https://thelisbonconcierge.com/en/guide/web-summit), read 2026-11-08). The 6 November article is newer.

## 2. Past years (Lisbon)

I found no first-hand wait figure for 2023, 2024 or 2025. What exists:

| Year | What is reported | Source (date read 2026-11-08) |
|---|---|---|
| 2016 (first Lisbon year, for context) | Opening night: arena declared full at 15,000 inside, many watched on a screen outside; next day, attendees got in "only after long queues". About 53,000 expected. | [Irish Times, 8 Nov 2016](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/technology/web-summit/web-summit-ticket-holders-not-able-to-get-into-packed-lisbon-venue-1.2859182) |
| 2022 | Entry "can take a while" in the first couple of days; "the queue backs up into the plaza" at the FIL and the arena. Pick up the badge "right after you clear customs". Non-EU passport control at the airport has a lengthy line. | Rob Pegoraro (public byline), [26 Oct 2022](https://robpegoraro.com/2022/10/26/a-little-lisbon-and-web-summit-advice/) |
| 2022 | Ticket exchange at the venue "quite crazy, especially on the opening day (1 hour and more)"; at the airport "manageable". Metro ticket machines at Oriente a long wait after day 1. | [freshestweb, 13 Nov 2022](https://freshestweb.com/en/blog/web-summit-tips-tricks-guide/) |
| 2024 | A travel blogger said badges could be collected right after customs. Registration opened Saturday 9 November in two places. No wait figure found. | Pegoraro 2022 again for the tip; the 2024 partner page is behind a Google login, so not read |
| 2025 | Badge pick-up on the first night or first morning "can take time due to the sheer number of attendees"; the airport tent "usually has no queues"; the arena closes when full on opening night. No date on the page. | [FusionAuth blog](https://fusionauth.io/blog/fusionauth-web-summit) |

What changed year to year: the airport desk has been there since at least 2022, and the 2025 and 2026 guides name the same two points. The crowd is about the same size (71,386 attendees in 2025 per one listing; about 75,000 expected in 2026). I found nothing that says the process got faster or slower.

Busy hours, from the 2026 guides (all third-party, advice rather than measurement): Tuesday morning, with trains "very full between 08:30 and 10:00" and "arrive early or after 10:30" ([esimpass, undated, covers 2026](https://www.esimpass.com/en/events/web-summit)); "pick up your badge on 9 November" ([Navan, updated 28 Jul 2026](https://navan.com/blog/web-summit-guide)); Monday afternoon is the recommended collection time in esimpass.
