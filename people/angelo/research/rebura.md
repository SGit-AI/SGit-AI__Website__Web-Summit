**Who they are and what they do**
- An AWS Premier Tier Services Partner, "100% focused on AWS": migration, modernisation, data, AI/ML, engineering on demand and a managed service with 24/7 support and free FinOps. It cites 12 AWS competencies and 90+ certifications. Source: [rebura.com/uk/en/about-us.html](https://rebura.com/uk/en/about-us.html).
- Acquired in 2024 by Westcon-Comstor, a technology distributor; the site says Rebura.com is operated by Westcon International Limited. Sources: the same page and the footer of [rebura.com](https://rebura.com/).
- Vision: "to be the go-to partner for AWS, its customers, and partners". Same about page.

**Recent public news (its news page, which shows relative ages, so dates are approximate)**
- About 17 days before 2026-11-08: named an OpenAI Select Partner.
- 22 September 2026: its own research says only 1 in 4 European businesses have fully defined outcomes for their AI initiatives.
- About a month before: certified a Great Place To Work.
- About 8 months before: AWS AI Services Competency. About 9 months before: a reseller agreement with Anthropic.
- Source for all: [rebura.com/uk/en/news.html](https://rebura.com/uk/en/news.html).

**Why it matters for RiskMandate and PrompTugal.news** (my reading)
- A partner that builds and runs customers' AI and AWS estates is where agents are given cloud permissions and model access: a direct fit for mandate limits. Its own survey (outcomes undefined before AI is adopted) is a ready story angle.
- Resells Anthropic and is an OpenAI partner, so a possible voice on governing agents across vendors. Pairs with the AWS brief.
