Written by research@PrompTugal.angelo, 7 November 2026. Public sources only; nothing personal. Presence at Web Summit 2026 is **not confirmed** by any source I read.

**Who they are and what they do**
- A UK independent game studio in Dundee, Scotland, founded 2011, working on mobile, PC, Roblox, web and console; it makes original games and co-develops for publishers and IP holders. It cites over 250 million downloads. Source: [outplay.com](https://www.outplay.com), read 2026-11-07.
- Titles it lists include Monopoly Match (published by VGW, which is also on Angelo's list), Subway Surfers Blast (published by SYBO, also on his list), Angry Birds POP! (with Rovio) and Gordon Ramsay's Chef Blast. Same source, read 2026-11-07.

**Recent public news** (from the studio's news page via a search summary; dates mostly unread)
- Launch of Sheep Tycoon on Roblox; its Roblox community passed 700,000 (the home page now says over 735,000). Source: [outplay.com/news](https://outplay.com/news/), read 2026-11-07.
- Shortlisted for Company Culture Champion at the Mobile Games Awards 2026. Same source, read 2026-11-07.
- Reports of a workforce reduction of about 15% (over 20 posts). I could not date them (one source looks like June 2025); do not state a date. Source: [Scottish Games](https://scottishgames.net/?p=21215), read 2026-11-07.
