## What writer@ prepares (one page, phone-readable)

1. **Lead A, the interview.** Questions on agentic governance in construction:
   - where agents already touch the work: tenders, scheduling, compliance documents, site reports;
   - who answers when an agent's output is wrong: liability across contractors and subcontractors;
   - approval steps (human sign-off) and audit trails;
   - safety-critical decisions agents must never take;
   - regulation (the EU AI Act, construction standards).
   - Ten questions at most, the best three marked, with a closing one: "what would you need to see to trust an agent with X?"
   - Also: how the interview becomes a PrompTugal.news piece, and the consent line to say on the record ("may I quote you and publish this on PrompTugal.news?").
2. **Lead B, the pitch in three lines.** Caution is the right instinct. Governance (bounded permissions, every action logged and signed, a human approving what matters) is what lets a cautious sector start small. The interview would be about that caution, not about selling agents.
3. **Lead C, one light question**, if they meet again: what an assistant for creators should never do on its own.

Angelo sends nothing from this sheet himself without reading it; publishing an interview is his call (tier 3).
