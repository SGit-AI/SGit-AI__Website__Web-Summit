Angelo, 8 November: "I have nothing booked yet, I need clarity on what is the recommended workflow, incorporating what others are doing." Then: "Go on all fronts."

## What the team already runs

- **Silvio's outreach** (people/silvio/outreach/): 50 ranked companies; agents draft emails into Gmail, Silvio sends each one; Dinis approves claims about RiskMandate; small batches; every email says an agent drafted it. Goal: visits to the stand, **A1-30, Thursday 12 November**.
- **Everyone's /meet/ page**, reached from the QR on a shirt or card. Angelo's is /meet/angelo/; booking on it waits on security's review (WS-119).
- **Dinis's X-rays** (/xray/): companies profiled as openers.
- **The CRM**: public facts about companies; Angelo's actions in the private one.

## The workflow

1. **Before.** research@ briefs his companies (people/angelo/research/requests.md, his order). He contacts the ones he knows well himself. Cold ones follow Silvio's process (an agent drafts, Angelo sends: tier 3, each one), agreed with Silvio first so no company hears from two of us.
2. **A meeting is agreed.** One line to the desk, or in requests.md under "Meetings": company, day, time. research@ refreshes the brief 48 hours before; crm@ records the action in the private CRM.
3. **At the summit.** People scan his QR to his meet page; he opens the brief (and the company's X-ray, if there is one) on his phone.
4. **After.** Two lines to the desk: who, what was said, what next. crm@ updates the CRM; a follow-up is drafted for him to send; a story idea goes to writer@ if there is one.

No person's contact detail is ever written in this repository; a person appears only in their public professional role.
