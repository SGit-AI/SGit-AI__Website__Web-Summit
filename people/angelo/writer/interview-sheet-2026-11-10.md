## A. The interview (exhibitor, construction)

**Before you start, on the record:** "May I quote you, by name and role, and publish this conversation on PrompTugal.news? You can see the piece before it goes out."

1. ★ Where do AI agents already touch your work today: tenders, scheduling, compliance documents, site reports?
2. Which of those tasks does an agent finish on its own, and which does a person always sign off?
3. ★ When an agent's output is wrong, who answers for it: you, the main contractor, the subcontractor or the software vendor?
4. What does your audit trail look like? Could you show a client exactly what the agent did, and on whose approval?
5. Which safety-critical decisions must an agent never take, whatever it is told?
6. How do you limit what an agent can reach: drawings, budgets, supplier data, people's records?
7. The EU AI Act requires that high-risk systems can be overseen, overridden and stopped by a person (Art. 14). Is that how you already work, or does it change things?
8. Which construction standards or client contracts already say something about AI, and what is missing?
9. What went wrong, or nearly, when you first let an agent loose?
10. ★ **Closing:** what would you need to see to trust an agent with a site safety report, end to end?

## B. The three-line pitch (VPS sector, cautious)

- Your caution is the right instinct: agents in development should earn trust, not get it by default.
- Governance is what lets a cautious sector start small: bounded permissions, every action logged and signed, a person approving what matters.
- The interview would be about that caution, not about selling agents.

## C. One light question (AI assistants for creators), only if you meet again

"What should an assistant for creators never do on its own?"

## Sources (read 9 November 2026)

- EU AI Act, Art. 14, human oversight: high-risk systems must let people understand, override and halt them; the page gives 2 December 2027 for Annex III high-risk obligations (Art. 113). https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/14/
- EU AI Act, Annex III point 2: safety components in critical infrastructure (road traffic, water, gas, heating, power) are high-risk. https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/
