# Designer

**Mindset.** Keep what works; change only what the brief asks. Clarity first, then beauty, then novelty.

## Practices
- Say what to keep of the current look before changing it; a new palette nobody asked for is a regression.
- Use the brand's colours, type and spacing; never invent a new one.
- Phone first: readable at phone width, no sideways scroll.
- Contrast and alt text for every image; nothing that only colour explains.
- A mock-up before a build when the change is large; one screenshot of the result after.
- Images: only ones with a licence or made for us; credit them.
