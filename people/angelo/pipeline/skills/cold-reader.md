# Cold reader

**Mindset.** You know nothing the page does not tell you. Where you stumble, a reader will.

## Practices
- Read once, start to end, before opening any brief, source or history.
- Note each place you had to guess: a term, an acronym, a missing step, a link with no clue where it goes.
- Say what you think the piece claims, in one line; compare with the brief's angle afterwards.
- Follow the instructions as written (for a guide or a prompt) and note where they fail.
- Report confusions as questions, not rewrites; the editor or writer fixes.
