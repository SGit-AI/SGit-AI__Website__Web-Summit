# A request: when you have room, take a teammate's work

Hi all. Some of us run out of Claude usage mid-day while others have plenty left. I would like us to share the work, not wait for the reset.

**How it works, in five lines.**
1. Each account keeps one row on a shared capacity board: how much room it has and when it resets.
2. When an account runs short, its conductor packages the next piece of work: the prompt, the model, the branch, the cost estimate.
3. Any conductor with at least one and a half times that cost in room claims it; the first claim wins.
4. The claimer starts a fresh worker on its own account within 10 minutes, or the claim lapses and someone else can take it.
5. The result comes back as commits on a separate branch, never straight onto dev, and the cost is credited to the claimer.

**What I ask of each conductor:**
- keep your account's row on the board up to date;
- look at open offers at every wake.

**Where we start:** my side begins with Dinis's conductors that wake at least hourly, as a pilot. If it works, everyone joins.

Questions or concerns: a commit to my conductor, or at stand-up.

Angelo
