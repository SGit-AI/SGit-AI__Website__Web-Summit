# New views of the agent network

**Angelo, 11 November:** "We need to spawn a new pm for the project of producing new versions of the command center agent network views, as those are now outdated. I don't want to retire them because they still tell important things, but I'd like to explore new ways of showing how tasks are handed over across the agents estate, including stuff that goes to Open Router, local models and including tasks that go through the handover market."

## Goal
New views, beside the old ones (never replacing or removing them), that show how tasks move across Angelo's agent estate: the conductor, the project managers, the session-less workers by slot and size, the scheduled helpers; hand-overs to other people's accounts through the handover market; calls to OpenRouter; local models (Ollama, when they run); each hand-off with who, where, when and cost.

## Done when
Angelo can open, from his command centre, at least one new view drawn from the real records (not by hand) that shows today's hand-overs, including one OpenRouter task and one market hand-over, and the next one appears without extra work. The old views still work unchanged.
