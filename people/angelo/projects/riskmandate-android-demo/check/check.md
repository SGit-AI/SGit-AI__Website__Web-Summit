## Findings (none blocks publishing)
1. **Slow page after a failure (plausible; needs a device to confirm).** After one error the watchdog reloads every 10 s from the start of each retry. Back-off only grows on a new error. If the page takes more than 10 s to load on slow summit Wi-Fi, each retry may be cut off by the next. This holds unless WebView reports the cut-off load as an error, which a device test would show. Fix later: skip the retry while a load is in progress and let the 45 s stall check cover it. `XrayPolicy.kt`, `XrayWatchdog.tick`.
2. **WebSockets and service workers are not filtered.** `shouldInterceptRequest` does not see these requests, so "everything else is refused" is true for page fetches only. Low risk, since only pages on web-summit.sgit.ai load. Fix later: a `ServiceWorkerController` client, and say so in the policy's comment. For security.android.
3. **`Features.kt` is the conductor's file** ("sessions do not edit this file"). The patch adds `XrayFeature` to it. The publish commit must name conductor.android@promptugal.ai.dinis in For:.
- Known and accepted in build.md: stand mode is off for `xray` (patch 0002 waits on Angelo); on WebView before 110, opening the site screen clears the demo key; not tried on a real device.
