### Monday 9 November, morning (the summit opens): what holds against a tricked agent

> Web Summit opens today. One question I'd like every team running agents to ask: if your agent were tricked by an email it read, what would actually stop it?
>
> A rule in its instructions won't. A setting its own account can switch off won't. A check after the fact finds out, but stops nothing. Only a boundary does: something enforced above the agent, out of its reach. A token that can't push to main. A mail scope that can't send.
>
> RiskMandate's open model for describing one agent (its grant, its mandate, the gap, and what really stands in the way) is free to use: https://riskmandate.ai/agent-behaviour-policy.html
