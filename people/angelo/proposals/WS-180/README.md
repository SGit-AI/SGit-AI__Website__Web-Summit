## Why

The back office was Dinis's idea, at the 6 November 12:00 stand-up: *"we need to find people to operate a backoffice, they need to support operations from outside the summit"* (WS-180). This is Angelo's proposal for it. In his words: *"The idea was Dinis's, I want to proactively offer a proposal."*

**Purpose:** a few people working remotely do three things:
- support the team on site whenever they ask;
- keep PrompTugal.news full of good articles and content through the summit;
- in return, get real experience of running agents.

## Who

Volunteers, who get hands-on experience of managing agents in return. Angelo's view: *"I suspect voluntary in exchange for getting hands-on agentic management experience."* Whether it is paid or voluntary, and who is asked, are for Dinis to decide. No names here: names go in the private vault.

## What they do, and what they do not

| | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Direct requests** from people on site | Whoever on site needs something sends a short note. The back office does it and replies. |
| 2 | **Handovers:** work people on site have no time for | Articles, research, materials, written up from the handover note. |
| 3 | **Their own articles and content** for PrompTugal.news | Through the newsroom's pipeline: its review, fact checks and publishing queue. |
| 4 | **Coordination for the team on site** | Arranging meetings and shifts, collections and reprints, sorting out clashes, chasing what is late. They work as people, using the agents' coordination board (WS-181) as their tool. The agents keep the board. |

They do all of it with their own small team of agents.

**What they do not do:** anything the agent teams already do by themselves. That means no watching runs, sorting inboxes, drafting follow-ups or keeping the coordination board. Angelo again: *"I don't think we should give backoffice people task agentic teams can take care of by themselves."*

## How requests reach them

The person on site writes a short handover note: what is needed, by when, and where it should go. The agents set up the channel (one place, which both the back office and the team on site can see). Anything urgent goes to the person's phone; the rest waits for handover at a stand-up.

## What each person needs

- **Accounts:** Workspace email, Claude and GitHub, through the team guide (about an hour).
- **A starter agent team:** from the agent setup guide, with its own token and an ABP.
- **Access:** read access to both repositories, and write access only where their work goes (the newsroom's desks). No admin.
- **To be part of the team:** the team group, and the coordination board.
