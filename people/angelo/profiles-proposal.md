## The problem

At Web Summit, people find us in three places, and the one they see most says the least:

| Where | Who sees it | Today |
|---|---|---|
| `/meet/<id>/`, where the shirt and card QR codes land | Everyone who scans us | The RiskMandate paragraph, the videos and the person's line. It says "Who Angelo is" but never says it. Booking and "talk to my agent" are "coming this week" |
| `/team/<id>/` | The team, and the curious | Mostly "to decide" and "to ask" |
| LinkedIn | Everyone who looks us up after a conversation | Whatever each of us wrote before; nothing matches the site |

The source for all three already exists: **the card** (WS-08, the five questions at the end of each brief). Only Jose has answered his (`people/jose/card.md`).

## The proposal

1. **One source, three outputs.** Each person's conductor asks the five card questions, one at a time (five minutes), and writes `people/<id>/card.md` in the person's words. From it, it drafts the person's meet-page block, their team profile and their LinkedIn text, and the person approves each one.
2. **A "who I am" block on every meet page:** one line of role in RiskMandate's terms, three "talk to me about" topics, who to see instead for what you don't do, and (if Dinis allows it) a link to the person's LinkedIn.
3. **The same LinkedIn kit for all seven,** each person filling in their own parts:
   - **Headline:** `<role> at RiskMandate · <their line>`.
   - **Banner:** one design for all seven, in each person's own colour (the shirt's): *Web Summit Lisbon · 9 to 12 November · Meet us*. designer@web-summit.dinis or designer@web-summit.indi.
   - **About:** two lines on what you do for RiskMandate (what LinkedIn shows before "see more"), then the RiskMandate paragraph, the same for everyone (WS-15, approved by Nimay, WS-66).
   - **Featured:** the person's `/meet/<id>/` page and the 53-second video.
   - **Experience:** one RiskMandate entry, with the same company description for everyone.
4. **"Talk to my agent" without waiting:** the feedback pane's public mode (WS-74, `?feedback=on&to=<agent>`) can replace "coming this week" once each person has a lane, after security's WS-119 is settled. A person answers every message, never the agent.
5. **The claims check:** before anyone changes their LinkedIn, an agent checks each claim against the site and its sources (for example, the stand on Thursday 12 November). Our profiles say nothing the site doesn't.
6. **The email signature:** the same pieces (colour, mark, line, a QR to the meet page). Angelo's is the first.
