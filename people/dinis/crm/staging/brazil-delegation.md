# The Brazilian delegation at Web Summit 2026 (public summary, draft for ws-team-crm-public)

Read 5 November 2026 by crm@web-summit.dinis. Public facts only, each with its source.

- **Who runs it:** ApexBrasil (the Brazilian Trade and Investment Promotion Agency, a LIS26 event partner,
  [appearance](https://websummit.com/appearances/lis26/312753b4-1ff2-4859-a8d1-07e1e3653da8/apexbrasil)) with Sebrae
  Startups; supported by Serpro, Embratur and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs through the embassy in Lisbon
  ([SAPO](https://sapo.pt/artigo/brasil-traz-mais-de-200-empresas-para-o-web-summit-2026-6aeb62e38c34e87b49bc84b7)).
- **How big:** about 200 companies with a 324 m2 space in the MEO Arena (Executive Digest, 4 Nov, as captured by
  researcher@web-summit.vicky; [Link to Leaders](https://linktoleaders.com/brasil-traz-200-empresas-ao-web-summit-para-conquistar-investidores-europeus/)).
  Jornal Económico breaks it down as 99 in Web Summit's programme, 90 from partner bodies and 50 from innovation
  ecosystems ([Jornal Económico](https://jornaleconomico.sapo.pt/noticias/brasil-leva-cerca-de-200-empresas-ao-web-summit-para-captar-investimento-europeu/)).
  Sebrae, on 30 September, expected about 300 startups once its national selection (100, 40 of them with travel paid),
  innovation hubs (up to five each) and 12 state missions are added
  ([Agência Sebrae](https://agenciasebrae.com.br/inovacao-e-tecnologia/sebrae-e-apexbrasil-anunciam-startups-selecionadas-para-o-web-summit-lisboa-2026/)).
- **The official list:** ApexBrasil publishes its approved companies by CNPJ (company registration number) and status
  only, no names ([ApexBrasil, EE-003616](https://crm-apps.apexbrasil.com.br/orgbdeab873/inscricao-eventos/evento/web-summit-lisboa-2026/0714952e-98da-f011-8543-7ced8db0f987)):
  188 rows at 28 August 2026, 105 confirmed, 26 awaiting confirmation, 33 on the waiting list, 24 withdrawn
  (`apexbrasil-approved-list.json`).
- **Named so far:** the 11 Brazilian startups on Web Summit's
  [featured startups](https://websummit.com/startups/featured-startups/) page (`brazil-companies.json`). One, Hablla,
  is matched by its CNPJ to ApexBrasil's list (confirmed). The others are Brazilian startups at LIS26, not yet
  matched to the mission.
- **AI agents (from each company's own Web Summit pitch):** builds conversational AI agents: Hablla (AI across
  WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, email and phone) and Raras Health (its assistant Rara talks to 300+ families a week on
  WhatsApp). Uses AI, agents not stated: AI Infinity, Just Travel, Mobile Brain. Not stated: FIDO, Fretefy, naPorta,
  Olá Doutor, ProHound, Rapidoc.
- **Also in the CRM already:** Sebrae Startups, Serpro (session "Brazil's blueprint for cyber resilience"), the
  Government of the State of São Paulo, ApexBrasil.

**Not done, and why:** naming the other ~130 confirmed or awaiting CNPJs needs a lookup of each in the public CNPJ
register (brasilapi.com.br answers plain requests). Bulk lookups were held back in this run and wait for Dinis's
word. Web Summit's full attendee directory sits behind its login (attend.websummit.com).
