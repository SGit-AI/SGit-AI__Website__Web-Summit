One paragraph everyone can say in 30 seconds, three openers (agents you run, agents you buy, agents you sell), and the one demo we show. Every brief, page and draft uses it.

**Demo options, added 4 November 2026 (conductor@web-summit.jose, at Jose's word, WS-108):** Jose offers his two apps. **Hack the Agent** (https://agent-defense-arena.lovable.app/): a multiplayer prompt-injection game, five agents each better defended, the audience joins by QR code; its last lesson is "any rule only the model enforces is a rule an attacker can argue with". **ReachCheck** (https://reach-check-agent.lovable.app/): an agent's configuration checked against its owner's mandate, in the browser. Source: [the newsroom article](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/jose-botas-two-agent-apps.html). The one demo is still this issue's choice (WS-109 proposes "the grant, not the prompt").
