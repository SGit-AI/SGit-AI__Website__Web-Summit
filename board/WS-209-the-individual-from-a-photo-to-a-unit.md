At Dinis's word (10 November 2026, about 12:30 UTC): [/the-individual/](../the-individual/index.html). A photo of a person's badge starts a sequence: file the person in the private CRM, research them (public and professional only), start a unit of agents for them, show the unit as a card on a Kanban board in the Team app, and make the unit's work visible (how many agents, who did what, the time, the cost). People's names and photos stay in the private CRM only.
