Angelo, 3 November: there are two onboarding workflows, not three. One is the RiskMandate team for Web Summit (WS-124). The other is **everybody else**: Daniela, Sarah, customers, and the team itself after the summit if we build our own agentic workflows. Its rule is **ownership**: everything is set up in the person's own accounts and is theirs to keep and use indefinitely, with nothing tied to Web Summit or to this repository. A dashboard or reference page here is optional and only a mirror.

The guide goes from high level to technical detail, so it handles each person's variations:

1. **Overview:** what an agentic workflow is, what they will own, the minimum to start, and how long it takes.
2. **Decisions:** the few questions that send each person down the right branch: new or existing accounts; which providers (Google or Microsoft, GitHub or another host, which Claude plan); a light setup or a locked-down organisation (SSO, admin approval for apps and connectors, blocked bots); personal or company-owned.
3. **Technical detail, per branch:** connecting Claude to email and calendar, Claude Code to their repository, keys and vaults, schedules, and behaviour policies (WS-34). Each step says how to check it worked and what usually goes wrong.

**Done looks like:** the guide published on the site, linked from the start page as the path for everyone outside the team, and tried end to end by its first user. Daniela is the likely first user, depending on the decision about her setup on the 3 November 13:00 stand-up (her brief currently follows the team's template, with `@web-summit.daniela` agents). Related: WS-59 and WS-60 (Daniela), WS-124 (the team's workflow), WS-35 (help with setup).
