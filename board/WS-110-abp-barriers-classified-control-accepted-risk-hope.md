From the newsroom article "The behaviour policy is the business logic": a rule only the agent enforces is hope. For each ABP in content/abp/, mark every barrier: **control** (enforced outside the agent: a permission, a gate check, branch protection), **accepted risk** (named and owned), or **hope** (the agent is asked to behave). One team table on /abp/, with counts. An honest live demo of RiskMandate's "control the gap".

Example: "never push to main" was hope until WS-105 (branch protection) makes it a control; it failed once (2 November).
