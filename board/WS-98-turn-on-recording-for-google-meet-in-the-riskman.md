The stand-ups could not be recorded. In the Google Admin console (admin.google.com): Apps, Google Workspace, Google Meet, Meet video settings, Recording: turn it on for the organisation. Recording needs a Workspace edition that includes it (Business Standard or above); check the edition first. Then the recordings land in the organiser's Drive, ready for the meeting debriefs (WS-96).

**5 November, from the 12:00 stand-up:** "if Dinis is not on the call we don't have the tool to transcribe the meeting". Until recording or Gemini notes work for whoever organises the call, a note-taker is named at the start of each stand-up (WS-96, Capture).
