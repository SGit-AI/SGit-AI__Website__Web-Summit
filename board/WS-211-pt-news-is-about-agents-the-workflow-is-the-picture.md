At Dinis's word (10 November 2026, about 12:40 UTC). The full guidance, close to his words: PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website `board/guidance/2026-11-10-pt-news-is-about-agents.md`.

- pt.news becomes *the* place to learn about agents.
- Interviews ask what the company does **with agents**. Each links to the company's GAMMA-Ray card, made before or after the interview.
- One workflow, built on [GAMMA-Ray](https://gamma-ray.sgit.ai/) and [FACT-Ray](https://gamma-ray.sgit.ai/fact-ray/): it maps where a team, a product or an agent is.
- The workflow and its picture are one: **a visual virtual newsroom**. It shows where the human, the researcher, the journalist and the agents come in, and every hand-off (Indi's interview, copied by hand into the site, then picked up by an agent). Readers see it on each piece, and we use it to run ourselves.
- A full redesign of pt.news, led by the interviews, the summit now and the scores. It recycles a direction we already have.
- The same flow as [the individual](../the-individual/index.html) (WS-210).
