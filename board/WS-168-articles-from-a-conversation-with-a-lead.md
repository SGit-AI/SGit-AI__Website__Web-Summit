From the stand-up of 5 November, 12:00 (Angelo, Jose, Silvio and Vicky), in Angelo's notes: "what's the process to write articles on spot when or after we talk to a potential lead?" No process exists yet.

**To settle:** who captures the conversation (a voice memo to the person's own agent, as WS-61 proposes); what may become public (only what the lead agrees to, or public facts about their company), and what stays private (the person, the details, the follow-up: the CRM vault, WS-23, WS-24, never this repository); who writes and who publishes (the newsroom, WS-14 and WS-141); and how fast (same day?).

**Done looks like:** one short page every person can follow at the summit: speak to a lead, record a memo, and by evening the follow-up is drafted (private) and, if the lead agreed, an article is drafted for the newsroom. Related: WS-61, WS-14, WS-141, WS-29.

## Proposal from Vicky, 5 Nov

Instead of writing articles on the spot, a live demo at the stand: the person talks to a Claude app project on our phone, and the agent makes a private page or infographic about their own agents' reach, mandate and gap, in about 5 to 8 minutes. Articles follow later, only if the person agrees; an interested person goes to the leads group, where Nimay or Dinis follows up.
