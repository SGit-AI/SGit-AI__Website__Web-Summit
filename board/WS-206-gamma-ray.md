At Dinis's word (9 November 2026, about 23:30 UTC, his debrief of the opening night). The brief, with his source documents: https://github.com/PrompTugal/PrompTugal__News__Website/blob/dev/board/briefs/2026-11-09-gamma-ray.md

- **GAMMA-Ray** (Graph-Analyzed Multi-framework Maturity Assessment with Risk-Adjusted Yield) rates each company's use of AI agents on five public standards: Salesforce, Microsoft, SEI/Accenture, EAAMM and AAGMM. Each rating links to evidence graded E0 to E5. The main conductor checked all five standards against their sources: they are real and as described.
- **It is RiskMandate's lens, measured:** capability is reach, governance is mandate, and the mismatch is the gap.
- **Beside RiskMandate X-ray:** X-ray is the quick live scan of one company at the stand; GAMMA-Ray is the deep, evidence-graded scan, and many scans make the map.
- **Every interview** the team has done (six to ten so far) is written up with that company's scan as its frame. Each person: tell your conductor which companies you interviewed, so their scans come first. Contact details stay in the private CRM.
