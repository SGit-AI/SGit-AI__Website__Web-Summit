**Run 1 done** (4 November, the C1 cards on /print/, site v0.2.265): `people/indi/synthetic/cards-run-1/`. Everybody in it is invented; it is not user research.

- 5 personas, 34 steps, 51 findings: 6 blocking, 16 costly, 19 minor, 10 held.
- All five scanned the QR; none booked.
- Blocking: the QR opens on the "Add your key" box (measured, all seven pages); the card promises "Book a meeting" and "Talk to my agent" and the page says both are "coming this week" (measured); no email or other contact anywhere.
- Costly: no card says what RiskMandate does; no card gives a role.
- Held: 7 of 7 QR codes decode to the right page (measured); "RiskMandate, in short" convinced all five once they reached it.
