At Dinis's word (8 November 2026, about 23:45 UTC, with Indi): the product strategy for our three apps, on [/apps/](../apps/index.html) with its infographic.

- **The event app** (PT News, the Lisbon event): on the App Store (submitted). Light and privacy-first: add features only where they change no permission, no privacy answer and no attack surface. No experiments until a basic version is out.
- **The team app** (PT News Team): never in a store; the only app with private data (vaults, keys, secrets); all our tasks, activities, projects, calendars, feeds (RiskMandate's tasks too), where everybody is, what the agents do. The place for experiments.
- **The RiskMandate app:** public, not in a store for now; the website's demos, videos, workflows and materials, to hand to any customer, client or tester.

**Each person:** ask your conductor and agents to work on the app you want to be part of, and say which, `For: conductor@web-summit.dinis`.
