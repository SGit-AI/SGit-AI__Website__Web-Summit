The design sprint: shirts, hoodies and cards

Deadline: the end of today, Monday 2 November. Everyone votes; Sarah and the designer agent turn the winners into print files; Vicky orders. Mock-ups, not print files: they show the idea, not the final type. Tap one to see it full size.

The brief

  • Shirts that start conversations and book meetings. When people see us they should think "I want to talk to these people". Each line is a RiskMandate question or claim someone can't walk past.
  • A QR on every back and every card, to the wearer's own page (/meet/): who they are, what RiskMandate does, book a meeting, talk to my agent. The code never changes; the page behind it does.
  • A very different colour for each of the seven, so three of us together stand out.
  • Something to be proud of wearing: RiskMandate, Web Summit 2026, Lisbon, and our stand day, Thursday 12 November (the stand location goes on once we know it).
  • 3 T-shirts and a hoodie each, plus a business card. A plain backup (T7) in case the venue pushes back.

T-shirts

ConceptFrontBack
T1 Do you know what your agents are doing? A question nobody can answer with a yes; the back says the wearer can.
T2 My agents have a policy. Do yours? Says what RiskMandate is in five words and asks the question.
T3 Who's on the other side of your agent? For buyers of agents: the risk they haven't thought about.
T4 Ask me how I control my agents. An open invitation; works for anyone on the team, technical or not.
T5 RISK MANDATE: agents with a mandate. The brand, big: for when we want the name remembered more than a question.
T6 I brought 6 agents to Web Summit. True, playful, and a demo in itself: each of us runs an agentic team.
T7 The plain backup. If the venue pushes back on messages: the name on the chest, the QR on the back.
