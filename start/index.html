The steps, for you and your agent

1. Claude (L1). A dedicated Claude account or project. Paste the prompt from your brief into a new session. 2. GitHub (L2). Send your GitHub username to Dinis; accept the invitation to SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Web-Summit. Your agent clones it (branch dev) and pushes its first commit: your people/<you>/onboarding.json. 3. Email (L2). An address you can send from: your own, or a workspace account Dinis creates for you. 4. PKI key (L3). Register at pki.sgit.ai: a passkey, a key pair made in your browser, your GitHub account as evidence. Dinis, the anchor, verifies you. Commit your public bundle (never the private key) as people/<you>/key.json. Each of your agents makes its own key with pki-agent (node pki-agent.mjs keygen --name "<agent>@web-summit.<you>") and links to yours (node pki-agent.mjs operator <your fingerprint>). 5. Vaults (L3). Dinis seals the team's vault keys to your key; you open them with your key. The private vault holds what can't be public (meetings, contacts). 6. A schedule (L4). One scheduled session that pulls the repository each morning, reads your dashboard and the board, asks you for your pulse, and tells you what is waiting on you. 7. More agents (L5, L6). Name them by the convention, run them as sub-agents first, then give each scheduled agent its own session.

How we work

  • The website is the centre, for the humans and for the agents. Every page has a markdown twin (index.md) for agents, and llms.txt lists them all.
  • One repository, everyone commits. Each person has a folder, people/<you>/: your dashboard, your onboarding, your pulse, your card, your public key. The board is board/, one file per issue.
  • Names: <agent>@web-summit.<person>, for example pm@web-summit.vicky. The conductor that keeps this site is conductor@web-summit.dinis.
  • Commits are how agents talk to each other. Subject <name>: <what>; trailers Agent: <name>, Issue: WS-NN when it is about an issue, For: <name> for a message to someone. Read what is for you with git log --grep "For: .*web-summit.<you>".
  • Releasing: commit your source changes only, then AGENT=<name> admin/tools/release.sh "<one sentence>". It rebases, bumps the version, builds, runs the gate, pushes (retrying if someone pushed first) and checks the live site.
  • Issues: the board. Pick one up by putting your name on it; tag-team, and hand over in a commit with For:.
  • Your dashboard is yours. Make it the page you want to see when you wake up. We copy the best ideas into the main page.
  • Public forever: no contact details, no keys, nothing private in the repository. Private things go in the private vault.
  • The team's agents (conductor, journalist, pulse, security) run on a schedule in Dinis's account; the list.

The pulse

Three times a day (morning, midday, evening), your agent interviews you for two minutes, or you type, and commits people/<you>/pulse/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<morning|midday|evening>.md. Public, on the pulse page. The questions:

1. What is your understanding of where we are? 2. What is blocking you? 3. What should we be doing that we aren't? 4. What's working, and what isn't? 5. Any good ideas? 6. How are you feeling, in general?

The pulse agent reads them all and flags anything early. Later we do the same for the agents.
