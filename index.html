Why in the open

The team sells Agent Behaviour Policies, and the strongest proof is using them where people can see. So the work is public by default: public repositories, public vaults with published read keys, and a small private vault for what cannot be shared, such as contact details and anything said in confidence. The goal is that people understand Agent Behaviour Policies, use them, and know what the team does.

What exists today

  • This site, released from the dev branch with a tag on every release.
  • Six vaults: a public and a private one each for the CRM, the messages (Email-FS) and the task board (Issues-FS).
  • A team roster, and a briefing page for the agent that handles the operator's email.

What is still to come

The map of the companies at the summit, the summit news, and the page for who is going to be there. Each starts from the plan and nothing is published about a company or a person without a source, or their yes.
