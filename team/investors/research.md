# Round 1: the research behind /investors/

Public sources only, each with its link and the date of the source. Everything was read on 6 November 2026 unless it says otherwise. **Read** means the page was opened and its text read; **snippet** means it was seen only in a search engine's summary; **not confirmed** means it was looked for and not found or not reachable. Quotes are short; the rest is paraphrase.

## What the investor pages take from this

- **The stance is his, in his words**: profitable before raising, open source because technology is not the moat, execution as the moat, selling customisation, "usage and the sale, in that order". Each is linked to its article.
- **No numbers, no projections**: no valuation, round size, revenue, market size or price on any investor page. The earlier MyFeeds figures are not reused.
- **Honest gaps stay visible**: one item on sale, no paying customer of sgit itself, the company's status not confirmed at the official register, the entities to be settled.
