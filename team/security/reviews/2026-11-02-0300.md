## Findings

1. **Medium: the published site is not the validated site** (WS-85, for the dev agent). Since WS-83, `deploy-pages.yml` builds twice: once in `validate`, and again in `deploy`, where the result is published without running the gate. The two builds come from the same commit, but `build.mjs` is not proven deterministic, and a change to either job can drift apart unnoticed. Fix: build once, validate that tree, and upload exactly it (one job, or `upload-artifact` from `validate`). Also faster, which is the dev agent's mandate.
2. **Medium: the secret and contact-detail checks have gaps** (WS-86, for the conductor, who owns the gate). Verified on this tree:
   - `comms_check.mjs` (gate check 15) does not scan `content/`, where the briefs, articles and the journalist's reports about people are written; it reads only `.md .json .eml .txt` and skips every `index.md`.
   - Its API-key rule misses Anthropic keys: `sk-ant-api03-...` does not match `sk-[A-Za-z0-9]{20,}` (tested: `false`). It also misses `github_pat_`, `gho_`, `ghs_`, `xox*-` and `AIza` keys, and JWK private members.
   - The tripwire in `validate.js` (check 5) matches `BEGIN PRIVATE KEY`, `RSA` and `EC` only, not `OPENSSH`, `ENCRYPTED` or `DSA`.
   With nine people and their agents about to push from many sessions, these are the ones to close before they do.
3. **Low: hand-written HTML outside `content/` is now silently ignored** (in WS-85). `.gitignore` ignores `*.html` and `index.md` everywhere but `content/`. A source page written anywhere else (a design mock-up, a widget) is never committed, and CI cannot notice, because the file never reaches it; only the local gate before a release catches it (check 10). And check 10 exempts whatever `build.mjs` lists in `.generated.txt`, so the build decides what may be ignored.
4. **Low: the whole repository is published.** The deploy step copies the tree except `.git`, `.github`, `.sg_vault`, `content`, `admin/build`, so `people/`, `team/`, `board/`, `data/` and `admin/tools/` are on the web. All of it is public by rule, so this is by design; it does mean anything committed by mistake is published within a minute. No issue opened.
5. **Info, for Dinis: this role's own policy is out of date.** `team/security/ROLE.md` and `policy.json` (drafted by conductor.2 on 1 November, status "proposed", not agreed) describe a sub-agent of the conductor's wake, and `scope.writes` does not include `board/`, where the agents page tells me to open issues. I opened WS-85 and WS-86 on the board, as the agents page and CLAUDE.md say, and did not widen my own policy. Dinis decides.
6. **Info: the WS-83 approval.** WS-83 changed a CLAUDE.md convention; the record of Dinis's yes is the conductor's line in `board/WS-83-*.md` ("Dinis said yes"). I cannot confirm it from the repository; nothing contradicts it.
