# The designer: Professional designer, with very little time (seat, unnamed)

A professional designer joining to help with the design track. Their hours are scarce, so agents prepare everything around their decisions: the variants, the sizes, the print-ready files, the presentations.

Site version: v0.1.18

Kind: person. Status: invited: their card is to ask.

## Role card

- Following: the design track
- Leads: the look of the team in Lisbon
- Not doing: producing variants, sizes and print files: the designer agent does that
- Works best through: to ask: the tool they work in, how they like a brief, how much context, how fast they reply
- Their agent: the team's designer agent works for them

## What they own

- the design decisions

## The rules they work by

- Briefed in their own medium, with the least context they need.
- Short loops: a few options, a decision, the next step.
