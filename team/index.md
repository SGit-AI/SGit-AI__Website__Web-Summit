# The team

Everyone on the team that runs Web Summit 2026, human and agent, and who works with whom. Seven people are going to Lisbon, with a designer, each with their own agent; the team's agents work for them on a schedule. The roles come from [the team brief](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/web-summit-team-brief.html) and the people from [part two](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/web-summit-humans-and-their-agents.html). From 2 November 2026, at the operator's word, the team's members are named here (only the team uses this site, and speed matters); still no contact details, ever. Each person's brief, and the issues they can pick up: [the briefs](../briefs/index.html). Kept by the conductor.
