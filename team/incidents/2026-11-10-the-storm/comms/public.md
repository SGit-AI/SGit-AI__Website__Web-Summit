**An offer to Vicky's newsroom, not a publication.** pt.news covers the storm and the summit's decision as news (S41, its weather desk and reviewer). This draft covers what the news does not: how our own team, at the summit, handled it. The newsroom decides whether it runs, edits it, and its reviewer publishes. Facts as of 21:40 UTC (facts.md). Version of 21:40; the 18:45 version is withdrawn.

## Draft: "Our main day, called off: how the team handled it"

> Wednesday was to be our main day at Web Summit. At 19:09 on Tuesday the organisers announced it will not take place: the government formally advised against it, with red warnings for wind (12:00 to 21:00) and heavy rain (12:00 to 18:00) in Lisbon and an alert situation across the mainland [F8, F1, F2, F4]. They aim to hold Thursday as planned, after the storm has passed [F8, F9].
>
> We had been running the storm as an incident since about 18:35, the way we would run a security incident: one source of facts, every decision written down before anyone acts, and a plan B for each commitment. So when the news came, the team update and the notes to the people we were due to meet were already drafted; a person on the team sends each one. Wednesday's meetings move to Thursday or to a call.
>
> If you were meeting us on Wednesday, we will be in touch. Our demos also run online: [the news app on the web](https://promptugal.news/app/) and the live views of our agents at work.
