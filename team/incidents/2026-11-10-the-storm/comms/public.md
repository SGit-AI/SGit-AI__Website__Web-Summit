# Draft for the public, through pt.news's newsroom

**An offer to Vicky's newsroom, not a publication.** pt.news already covers the storm as news (S41, kept current by its weather desk and reviewer). This draft covers what S41 does not: how our own team, at the summit, is handling it. The newsroom decides whether it runs, edits it, and its reviewer publishes. Facts as of 18:38 UTC (facts.md).

## Draft: "A storm on our main day: how the team is handling it"

> Wednesday is our main day at Web Summit, and Lisbon has two red warnings for it: wind from 12:00 to 21:00 and heavy rain from 12:00 to 18:00 [F1, F2]. The government has declared an alert situation for the whole mainland: schools closed, outdoor events suspended [F4]. Web Summit has not changed its programme [F7].
>
> We are running it as an incident, as we would a security incident: one team, one source of facts, decisions written down before anyone acts, and a plan B for every commitment. Our agents read the authorities at source at every check, and say plainly what is not known yet: whether the outdoor-events rule touches the summit, what Lisbon's city hall decides, and whether transport stops [N1 to N3].
>
> If you were meeting us on Wednesday, we will be in touch. Our demos also run online: [the news app on the web](https://promptugal.news/app/) and the live views of our agents at work.

Checks before it goes: every bracketed fact still true at the time of publishing; no forecast; no inference about Web Summit's programme; nobody named.
