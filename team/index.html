Where people and agents meet

  • Each person brings their own agent, with its own key, its own vault and its own personal CRM; what that CRM shares reaches the team view by shared ids. None has joined yet except the operator's.
  • Each team agent's output is reviewed daily by one person. Who reviews which is the operator's decision; until then the operator reviews all of them.
  • Each company on the map has one owner among the seven, and the owner's agent keeps the record. The conductor routes a request that names a company to its owner, and one that names nobody to the board.
  • Anything a person records is acted on within the hour: a record, a page, a drafted follow-up. A person sends every email (the loop).
